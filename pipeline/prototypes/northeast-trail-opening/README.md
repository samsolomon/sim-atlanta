# Brookwood pedestrian tunnel

Private R16 mount, OSM 38981771 v13. Install beside the northbound Segment 2 trail fixture. Call `trailOpening(originalUncutGround, quad)` once on the R16 board. Append its slots to the existing board openings and draw the quads with a double-sided vertex-colour material. The tread uses the identical northbound station and offset sampler. The separate one-way vehicle tunnel 38981788 has its own floor, openings and geometry.

Both mouth positions follow the mapped tunnel endpoints. The June 2026 interior panorama shows the paved strip, flat roof, walls and lights on the east side looking north. The August 2019 south view establishes the rectangular headwall and its connection to the retaining bank. The July 2023 construction photo shows the bridge over GA 13 and the south mouth of the I-85 passage in the same frame. The finished paved trail opened in November 2023. The 2025 NBI record 000000012106700 describes I-85 over Atlanta Beltline at scene (1292.77, −6101.21), a single 10.4 m span and 56.3 m deck width. Item 54 gives reference N and numeric field 7.16. Applicability to the modern trail clearance is unverified. The fixture infers a 7.0 m clear height, 7.2 m inside width, 0.45 m roof and 6 m entrance transitions. The 14 ft path width is a Segment 2 design proxy. The roof follows the inferred trail grade. Lamp spacing, wall thickness and gravel margin widths are illustrative. Source imagery supports the presence and side of the lights.

Original earth higher than the roof is retained across the 78.57 m mapped bore. Terrain removal extends between the trail margins on the two 6 m approaches and widens to the inside walls. The upper ramp and freeway act as crossing controls at northbound stations 257.33, 282.64 and 310.77 m. The independent GA 13 bridge controls are at 151.87 and 164.63 m. Open trail grade and ground beyond these approaches belong to the path fixture.
