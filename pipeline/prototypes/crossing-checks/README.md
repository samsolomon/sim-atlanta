# Crossing calibration on assembled City faces

Run from the repository root with the project Python environment (NumPy and Shapely 2). Each export directory contains a low and high subdirectory. Supply Phone’s assembled world-space, draw-range exports with the original triangle labels, per-face sRGB colours and board owners. Use the wider north Peachtree export covering x −100..65 and z −5140..−4940 when checking both ground toes. Raw paths on Studio are shown below.

```sh
.venv/bin/python pipeline/prototypes/crossing-checks/check.py /tmp/four-city-study/step0 --expect baseline --out /tmp/crossing-before.json
.venv/bin/python pipeline/prototypes/crossing-checks/check.py /tmp/four-city-study/step0-beb9b2d0 --expect peachtree --out /tmp/crossing-landing.json
.venv/bin/python pipeline/prototypes/crossing-checks/check.py /tmp/four-city-study/step0-1d04eadc --expect combined --out /tmp/crossing-after.json
```

All modes use the same measurements and thresholds. A future independently corrected Capitol can use `--expect repaired`, which requires all three sites to pass. The mode selects the expected regression outcomes. A disagreement or missing input exits nonzero. With `--tier high` or `--tier low` the same comparison can run on a single completed export. All signed heights are in scene metres. A positive toe gap denotes daylight. Stations increase in source order. The JSON includes camera target x,z,bearing,tilt,distance for each registered failure.

* **17th Street.** OSM outline 1452444262 and the 37 cross sections in the City fixture locate the driving surface, walks, ramp mouths, five pier rows and board cut. For the two 17th decks on opposite sides of z = −4100 the centre travel band spans the loaded boards. The reference profile comes from class-17 pavement returns. The fascia, walk curbs, haunches, shaft shape and positions are modeled. The integral caps have no separately modeled bearing pads. [Reference description](../../../docs/seventeenth-street.md)
* **North Peachtree.** OSM 38978515 carries six lanes. The local model has 3.1 m walk strips centred 15 m to either side. The checker casts through the entire skew terminal row and along each side of the landing. The accepted 113.7 m by 34.0 m inventory bridge reports 10.05 m clearance to a highway. The detailed steel webs have vertical projected area, so their bottom **edges** are clipped to lower-road travel polygons. The railhead-to-road-top value is a model diagnostic. Physical rail clearance is unknown. [Source and inventory](../../../docs/brookwood-rail.md)
* **Capitol Gateway.** The baked street and walkway positions match source ways in the frozen south-band OSM snapshot. The source register stores class-2 bare-earth window counts and 25th/50th/75th percentile NAVD88 elevations in 2 m disks at 2 m stations from USGS Georgia Statewide B2 2018. The top of this urban cover is classified as ground. The source grade-change envelope propagates the interquartile endpoints of the nearest measured windows on the two limbs. It is a local comparison with the observed source spread. At a short landing the grade intervals shrink together on both sides. The missing physical section under the covered junction leaves its underside profile and true traffic clearance unresolved. The eight mapped lower movements in the crossing plan cannot take the top of the cover as their floor.

The source register for lower roads preserves the two registered travel edges at successive directed stations as emitted by the frozen carrier code. Numerical checks clip the actual triangles to those travel polygons. The shaft and shoulder check uses the local rendered ribbon allowance (0.7 m per side for links, 1.8 m otherwise). `pier-register.json` pins the twenty original shaft centres. The original has open-bottom shafts, whose four coplanar rim points define the base plane. A fitted bottom uses the actual drawn faces. Each clipped face-pair is affine so its signed extrema occur at polygon vertices. `areaM2` gives the union of the covered footprint. Exact overlaps with lanes and shoulders are reported independently.

The seam checker intersects each terminal band with actual facets and evaluates on each side of their breakpoints. It samples 0.5 mm inside the longitudinal and lateral borders, reports missing intervals and applies the approved 1 mm contact and 2 mm flush-step budgets. The separate walk-support probe follows centre and lateral tracks at quarter-metre spacing and distinguishes a walk’s 0.4 m shell underside from its top. Reported unsupported lengths are sampling estimates. Street-level manoeuvres, wheel transforms and streaming replays use Phone’s matched camera/clock records separately from this static geometry check.
