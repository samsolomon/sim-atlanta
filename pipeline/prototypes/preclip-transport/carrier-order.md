# Carrier and crossing order before fitting

The [frozen experiment](https://github.com/samsolomon/sim-atlanta/tree/476927f3ec483d01b2e654e09aab159f37702dd7/pipeline/prototypes/preclip-transport) remains reproducible, including its 9.835–10.438 m blind error. This subsequent classifier reads the identified axes, their topology and the existing deck catalog. It emits source-attributed fields before any vertical solve. A null field gives the reason for withholding it. Physical clearance is unresolved in all 24 records.

```sh
.venv/bin/python -B pipeline/prototypes/preclip-transport/carrier_order.py
.venv/bin/python -B -m unittest discover -s pipeline/prototypes/preclip-transport -v
```
