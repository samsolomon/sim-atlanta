Sim Atlanta

A miniature city you can watch: MARTA trains and buses where they really are, traffic as it usually runs at this hour, and the sky Atlanta has right now.

What you're looking at

The City view is a hand-built model of downtown, drawn like a tabletop diorama. The Map view zooms out to the region's rail: subway, streetcar, Amtrak and the freight lines that cross the state. Both show the same moment, Atlanta time.

Nothing here is for planning a trip. It's a window, not a timetable.

How true is it?

Every moving thing carries a label for how much we actually know about it:

  • liveMARTA reported this train or bus's position in the last few minutes.
  • scheduledWhere the published timetable says a train should be, used when there's no live report.
  • reportedPublished counts, such as how many freight trains use a line each day.
  • inferredWorked out from nearby data, such as traffic on a freight branch from the lines it joins.
  • illustrativeDrawn to look right, not measured: freight train movements, car traffic, and what the trains look like.

Live data only appears when the clock shows the real present.

Colophon

Built with Three.js and MapLibre GL, and served from a Cloudflare Worker that caches every live feed, so a busy day doesn't cost anything extra. The art direction follows a low-poly, ink-lined railway diorama: leaf-clump trees, thin silhouette ink, a colour grade with film grain, and tilt-shift. Trains and buses are modelled in code from published dimensions and reference photos, in the model lab.
