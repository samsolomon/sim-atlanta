# When a City expansion row counts as built

Apply this proposed acceptance checklist to each new row **and to the whole square delivered to a browser**. The Beltline-loop square is the first intended future use. This document plans the checks. Implementing them and building that square are separate work.

## Inputs and acceptance record

Before generating a row, freeze its identifier, City bounds, adjacent rows, projection, elevation datum, source vintages and expected coverage. Record source features that intersect the rectangle, including polygons and lines whose vertices all lie outside it. Assign stable source IDs and expected receiving layers. Record the generator revision, stage order, output paths and hashes, source counts, excluded IDs and reasons, and expected Low and High coverage. Keep source counts distinct from clipped pieces, tier-selected features, mounted objects and visible instances.

**Missing or empty fails by default.** When the source supports a true absence (for example, an area with no railway, scheduled service or golf course), record the bounded source query, date, zero count and ownership decision. A missing file, unchecked empty array, missing upstream source or intentionally unimplemented stock class is a coverage gap. A documented intentional absence or deferred layer should appear explicitly in the coverage report. An expected landmark or movement missing from the shipped scene blocks signoff.

## Layers and proposed automated checks

Run each row below on the final archive. Run the geometry, routing and visibility checks on both Low and High after mounting the built app. “Reported”, “scheduled”, “inferred” and “illustrative” describe individual attributes. Time-shifted checks use the timetable. Realtime arrivals belong only at the present clock. [Truth tiers](data-model.md)

| Dataset or derived layer | Source and truth tier | Generator or staging step | Failure witness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain, elevation and bounds | Pinned USGS lidar-derived DEM and metadata, reported elevations in NAVD88. Inferred interpolation and grade edits. | Bake the quantized grid and its manifest with square_board.py and downtown_terrain.py. Apply documented cuts, fill and ground yields. | Reject missing or empty grid, wrong byte length, NoData in bounds, mismatched datum, stale row origin, or unequal shared-edge samples. Probe actual Low/High triangles, including carved surfaces, at registered control points. |
| Roads, lanes, markings and levels | Directed OSM ways and turn/bridge/tunnel/layer tags, reported. Check strips and gore against dated orthophotos. Lane widths and hidden grades may be inferred. | Extract and clip context, preserve direction and source station, build the transport graph and render a common tread/stripe/traffic profile. | Compare source IDs, direction, lane intervals and lower/upper order. Fail lost recipients, an invented turn at a crossing, stacked stripes, a gap in a shared lane or a lane ending inside the board without a mapped cause. |
| Sidewalks, crossings and trails | Mapped paths, sidewalk-side and crossing tags plus registered imagery. Inferred widths and kerbs. | Preserve walk connectivity, clip crossing footprints, associate each segment with drawn ground or its actual bridge/landing, and draw crosswalks on the right carriageway. | If the source has walks or crossings, fail zero coverage. Trace both outer edges and the pedestrian route through deck joints, road mouths, tunnels and seams. Flag unsupported lengths, paint on the wrong level or missing receiver. Return first station and camera. |
| Bridges, retaining structures, tunnels and openings | FHWA/NBI or operator inventory, OSM outlines, USGS class-17 and exposed class-2 samples, dated side/underside photos. Inferred hidden stations and dimensions. | Register each inventory structure to its upper and lower carriers. Fit deck, girder, approaches, feet, bearings, parapets, cut or bore as applicable. Inventory matching also needs an unmatched-way and unmatched-inventory report. | Run full-width checks for step and seam, whole-footprint seating, underside clearance over actual road/rail, lane/shoulder overlap and open portals. Fail missing expected structures, closed bays or fake abutments at source splits. [Crossing limits and fixtures](connector-bridge-plan.md) |
| Rail infrastructure | Active track and route geometry from OSM, BTS/FRA network and crossings, operator information. Observed grade and inferred occluded segments. | Match track identities and active/disused status, bake continuous rail profiles and route/clip ownership across rows. | Fail missing expected track, reversed direction, false connection or missing continuation. Run wheel contacts and a complete head-to-tail replay through each internal seam and actual outer edge with the neighboring rows loaded in both orders. |
| Service, timetable and moving consists | MARTA/Amtrak GTFS trips, calendars, stops and shapes, scheduled. Freight stock and timing illustrative. Realtime only from the existing live adapter. | Run the pipeline GTFS and freight stages. Bind qualifying trips to the mounted track owner and use service-day clocks. | Require a known scheduled trip on each served line at a fixed active time and a non-service control. Fail missing eligible trips, duplicate train IDs, a disappearing consist, or a truth-tier mismatch. Register freight separately. |
| Buildings, heights and foundations | OSM or Overture footprints and parts with stable ID, reported tagged height/floors. Lidar-derived estimates or illustrative defaults where needed. | Extract buildings, join height/part records, seat foundations on the drawn terrain and deduplicate split or replaced footprints. | Compare per-row intersecting footprints and area with staged, loaded and drawn ownership. Fail missing buildings, duplicates, invalid heights, buried entrances and floating foundations. Record measured/tagged/estimated height counts. |
| Tree canopy and fallback | Local mapped canopy/crown pack and dated Atlanta canopy classification. Crown centres/sizes inferred where generated. | Merge the landscape pack after base context generation. Sample deterministic fallback only in verified coverage gaps, apply rail, road, trail, water and playing-surface exclusions, select by tier. | Fail a source-covered row with zero staged or mounted crowns. Check source, rejection, accepted, rendered and fallback counts by tier, stable positions and both sides of seams. Check normal camera after a cold and a warm load. An empty array can suppress fallback. |
| Land cover, water, parks and golf | OSM water/land-use/leisure and golf polygons and hole references, dated orthophotos. Mapped bounds reported, cut widths, bowl depth and daily pin placement inferred or illustrative. | Clip every intersecting polygon into every touched row. Stage water, ordinary ground, parks, cart paths, fairways, greens, fringe, tees, bunkers, bowls, hole pins and tee markers where present in source. | Compare clipped area to the source union, including adjacent rows. Check expected surface classes and unique flags by hole ID, tee pairs, bowls against drawn terrain and visible continuity from ordinary course cameras. A populated row south of a missing piece cannot satisfy this check. |
| Traffic paths and stock density | OSM signed road graph, FHWA HPMS AADT where available, observed lane allocation. Volume allocation inferred, all ambient cars illustrative. | Build freeway and ramp routes and separate arterial routes, connected across row limits. Declare the intended local-street coverage. Stage each fleet and its per-tier allocation. | For each row × road class × direction list eligible path-km, source/recipient IDs, assigned slots and visible moving counts at pinned clocks. Fail a promised class with zero routes or slots, or an unreachable movement. Compare a full-scene census with ordinary camera counts and run both branches of every tested fork. A train diagnostic cannot count cars on roads. |
| Landmarks and model overrides | Approved site list and accepted revisions, dated exterior views, approved footprints and known height/night uncertainties. | Include the model modules and row registration, resolve parked candidates and deduplicate generic substitutes. | Fail when an approved landmark does not mount by its footprint ID in the shipped bundle, even if generic buildings exist there. Capture normal-scale daylight and full-night on the same camera with neighboring massing visible. |
| Streaming, seams, cache and delivery | Row manifests, registered bounds, source continuation IDs, actual built bundle and fetched contexts. Implementation behavior is measured on the app. | Package all derived assets after final merges. Register the row for ordinary camera navigation and the explicit full-board action. Version mutable URLs or verify revalidation. | Cold-start on the adjacent row, pan normally and frame a cross-row feature while its far half is visible. Separately cold-start at an interior camera and choose Show full board. Fail missing/duplicate mounts, stale browser hashes or changed exposure on reload. Test cross-row buildings, ground, foliage, street/rail and moving subjects. Replay a warm cache previously populated by the last shipped build. |
| Cost and performance | Same machine, viewport, clock, quality and motion across baseline and candidate. | Measure final compressed data/JS, texture allocation, draws, triangles and frame times after visible geometry settles. | Fail zero-draw witnesses, exceeded project budgets or unexpected new textures. Keep paired Low/High evidence with the exact archive and browser-requested hashes. [Performance budgets](perf.md) |

## Brookwood acceptance examples

- **Canopy.** The initial assembly omitted 7,444 source crowns (4,067 Station and 3,377 South). A subsequent correct archive still looked empty on Air because the browser reused an older 410,001-byte Station context. Count source, staged and actual loaded crowns separately. The combined reference staged 3,931 Station, 3,363 South and 317 inferred r16 crowns after exclusions and selection.
- **Routes.** At Exit 86, northbound way 369529900 carried six positions into five through on 369529886 plus a right exit on 9238434. Both recipient ribbons were absent in the earlier traffic catalogue. Require both downstream movements to occur in the rendered app. At Deering a route-length change shifted the repeating car clock. Preserve the original miss alongside any re-identified car.
- **Course and row loading.** In 3b36f995 the approved golf payload resolves to South 1 flag / 2 bunker pieces, Station 6 / 22, and r16 2 / 7. Nine flags across three rows require an actual cross-row view. The r16 bounds start at z=−5700. The old loader checked whether the orbit target was within a 100 m halo, even during Show full board. A camera aimed at (400, −5200) could see northward without requesting that row. Record the row set, viewport and rendered course at the same instant.
- **Models.** The approved Piedmont corner garden, North Deck and tower geometry existed on a separate branch despite their absence in 3b36f995. Compare the accepted footprint/model list to the final mount. Gate a later Shepherd inclusion on Sam's approval.
- **Scope of traffic.** The 204 matched arterial runs in this fixture cover Midtown South/Central/Upper (31/52/69), Brookwood South (37) and Station (15, Peachtree). R16 has freeway and ramp stock. Its arterial allocation is empty. These rows have no ordinary neighborhood-street stock layer. The private north-study state field named cars selects one scheduled MARTA train. Use each road renderer's actual instanced vehicles for the census.

## How to run the future gate

1. The expansion owner registers every layer above and its expected coverage before generation. Include neighboring rows and all source movements that cross the outer boundary.
2. A future pipeline target should validate the source manifest and staged assets, emit per-layer counts and fail on missing inputs or unexplained empties. Use the same row manifest to select the contract, seam, structure and traffic fixtures.
3. Build an archive from a clean staging directory. Serve that archive through the same entry point as the recipient. Run Low and High ordinary navigation, full-board loading, contact checks and moving replays. Compare browser-fetched content with the archive as well as object counts and pixels with expected scene features.
4. Publish a compact per-row result with source revision, build SHA, any explicitly unserved class, signed first failure and camera, paired screenshots, and performance deltas. Mark the square ready only after cross-row acceptance. For Brookwood, include Piedmont at the corner and map distance, both Ansley halves, an r16 ordinary neighborhood view, the approved rail/walls, and the four highway marks.
