For each skew cross-section, sample 2019 USGS lidar on the two road margins, 18% in from either outside edge. Use the median of at least four class-17 bridge returns within 2.2 m, expanding to 3.5 m where needed. Exposed class-2 returns provide a fallback near the banks. Interpolate the crossfall to the two curbs and parapets. The initial implementation sampled rendered ground at the parapets, curbs and centreline. It eased those offsets into the measured grade over at least 7 m where space permits, allowing 4 m of transition per metre of vertical discrepancy. That transition ended within one third of the span and before any quad that crosses a board seam. The approach paint follows the connected street and pedestrian ways. Peachtree crosses a board seam on its measured grade. The Capitol deck clips at the square’s southern boundary before its far abutment.
