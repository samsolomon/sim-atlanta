# Woodruff Arts Center and High Museum of Art

The 126-vertex High Museum footprint connects several wings. The Stent wing faces Peachtree with a quarter-round glazed atrium, a radial skylight and the projecting former entrance ramp. The white Piano galleries face it across Sifly Piazza. The ground-level glazing, fine vertical panel joints and rows of roof scoops distinguish them from Meier's square-panelled galleries. The separate 68-vertex Memorial Arts Building footprint contains the long Peachtree colonnade and a higher performance volume toward Arts Center Way. The paved piazza follows local terrain. [High Museum footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/28961738) · [Memorial footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/28961767) · [Architect's campus description](https://www.rpbw.com/project/high-museum-expansion)

The landscape approach shows the scoop silhouette above the gallery walls. The elevated Peachtree angle resolves the radial skylight, the punched windows on the end wall and the ramp between two smaller projections. The roof layout was checked against Esri World Imagery centred at City (560, −3965). The historic night photograph of Memorial shows light in the lobby and on the tall piers beneath the cornice. The AJC night photograph of the High shows light beneath its rounded projection with much darker enamel above. The Peachtree account also describes light passing through the curved atrium. City lights the atrium, selected lobby bays and the ground-floor glazing. Colour, intensity and occupancy are illustrative. The historic photographs predate the expansion.
