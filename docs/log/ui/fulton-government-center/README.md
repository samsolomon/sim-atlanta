# Fulton County Government Center

The county lists its administrative offices and Board of Commissioners at 141 Pryor Street SW. Approach from Pryor and a sign over the small gabled portico identifies the Government Center. Behind it, the entry plaza rises toward four much larger glass gables. The portico, plaza and atrium are visible in a November 2025 panorama. The county rededicated the plaza, atrium and Assembly Hall in 2021. Across Pryor at 136 is the historic courthouse. The enclosed bridge bears the county name above the street between the two blocks. ## What changes around the block

- **Pryor.** The main tower ends in a tall stone gable framing a stack of projecting dark windows. The plaza side combines flush glass, a deep grid of punched openings, and the lower glass-and-metal portico. The county’s own close-up shows how the four atrium gables rise from one glazed wall, with white mullions and triangular trusses above a stone base. [Tower end and portico](https://www.flickr.com/photos/courthouselover/51872657093/) · [County atrium photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/fulton_county_info/3288201582/) · [View across Pryor](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7510229,-84.3912757&heading=302.41&pitch=17&fov=88)
- **Martin Luther King Jr. Drive.** The tower runs nearly the full block, with long bands of dark glazing, pale vertical divisions and a row of recessed ground-floor openings. The metal ridge runs parallel to the street. [MLK street corner, April 2026](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7514472,-84.3910275&heading=285.17&pitch=19&fov=88) · [Site aerial](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/export?bbox=-9394571.488101812,3995340.046599618,-9394369.200953368,3995542.333748062&bboxSR=3857&imageSR=3857&size=1000,1000&format=jpg&f=image)
- **Peachtree and Mitchell.** On Peachtree, two glazed atrium gables appear between the office tower and a lower stone wing. Viewed from Mitchell, small diamond windows punctuate the pale gable ends. Around the Pryor corner, the lower wing stands on a colonnade leading toward the glass bridge. The large county name is stacked on the bridge face seen from Mitchell. [Peachtree, March 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7516474,-84.3924648&heading=142.03&pitch=22&fov=88) · [Mitchell and Pryor, November 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7503137,-84.3918683&heading=1.05&pitch=13&fov=88) · [Street view beneath the bridge](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7507132,-84.3915751&heading=355.13&pitch=22&fov=88) · [County photograph of the bridge, cover](https://www.fultoncountyga.gov/-/media/FC-Justice-Presentation/Fulton-County-Operational-Update-for-10-18-23.pdf)

## What the footprints and lidar establish

Two Overture building records carry the Government Center name. The 108-vertex outline (`8ce87973-e33f-4f88-aa5d-2d557abf5bc5`) includes the office tower, atrium and lower wings. The second outline has 22 vertices (`4f4d729c-a11b-48de-937e-93af0c7916ec`) and covers the southwest Mitchell wing. Their baked heights were 53.7 m and 19.6 m. The historic courthouse and Justice Center Tower share a third outline (`0a4a8480-d8f4-40bf-9346-853c053cd064`) east of Pryor. The Carnes Justice Center immediately to their south has a fourth (`e17e6fe1-2da7-47ad-85f7-ef73084da73c`). City replaces the first two records together. [Footprints over lidar elevations](lidar.webp) · [Courthouse study](../fulton-courthouse/README.md)

The two Government Center polygons contained 19,596 and 3,866 sampled lidar returns respectively. The following heights are approximate metres NAVD88 from the 2018 Georgia statewide survey. The height above the sidewalk varies along each sloping street. [USGS Georgia Statewide Block 2, 2018](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)

## Evening light on Pryor

Fulton County’s November 11, 2026 exterior photograph shows green light behind all four atrium gables for Operation Green Light. The white grid stands out against the glass, and individual warm lamps appear along the stone base. A second twilight photograph shows the courthouse entrance opposite. The November 20 City comparison uses the warm entrance lights. Office occupancy and ordinary atrium brightness are illustrative.
