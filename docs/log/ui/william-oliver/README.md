# The William-Oliver Building at Five Points

At the Peachtree and Marietta corner, the William-Oliver has two warm masonry street faces in front of the much taller metal-and-glass tower at 34 Peachtree. The original City shell filled the 16-story building's full footprint up to a single roof. On the side away from the intersection, the roof survey drops almost to the neighboring shop roofs. [Condominium association history and archival photographs](https://thewilliamoliver.org/about/) · [Preservation easement and ornament description](https://easementsatlanta.org/portfolio-item/william-oliver-building/)

Photographs from Peachtree show paired sash through the center, single lights at the edges, terracotta spandrels and a bronze-colored canopy close to the shared wall. The Marietta view shows the other long street face. The model picks out the piers, chevrons, frieze and entrance in low relief. The photographer’s entrance detail records “1930” in the original metalwork.
