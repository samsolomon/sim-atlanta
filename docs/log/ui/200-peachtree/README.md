# 200 Peachtree

Davison’s 1927 department store faces Peachtree with two-story arched display windows, a pale stone belt, paired punched windows in red brick and a projecting dark cornice. The Ellis Street photograph shows the same arches returning around the corner. The present-day event and music entrances have canopies spanning individual bays. The venue’s archive records the earlier awnings. [Peachtree frontage](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:200_Peachtree_Building,_Atlanta,_GA_(40508249623).jpg) · [Ellis corner](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:200_Peachtree_Building,_Atlanta,_GA_(40508249323).jpg) · [Southern Exchange archive](https://www.southernexchangeatl.com/copy-of-about)

The Juke Joint night view shows a bright marquee and light through the revolving doors. A promotional close-up resolves colored neon and small bulbs. A verified full-night view of the entire roof and upper floors was unavailable. Their sparse lit panes are illustrative. The Overture ring is about 93 × 80 m and carries a 44.5 m height. In the 2018 USGS point cloud the Peachtree sidewalk is near 329 m NAVD88 and the front roof fields are near 365–367 m. Behind them stand taller enclosures around 370 and 377 m. The Carnegie Way addition steps down to about 355 m. The model follows this cross-section. The bay sizes, sill heights, storefront lighting, cornice and mechanical housing outlines are visual estimates.
