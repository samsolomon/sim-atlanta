# 75 Piedmont, the former Citizens Trust headquarters

Citizens Trust moved its headquarters to Piedmont and Houston (now John Wesley Dobbs Avenue) in 1969. The modernist steel-and-glass building housed the historically Black bank, with Italian marble columns around its original banking lobby. In 2007 the University System of Georgia acquired the building. Citizens Trust announced its headquarters move to 230 Peachtree in 2016 and closed its Piedmont branch on June 30, 2017. Georgia State now uses the building for offices, including its third-floor Employee Success Center. The university lists the building and its parking deck separately. ## Reading the building from Piedmont

From the south the cream-colored fins run the full height of the bronze office windows. A blue flame marks the narrow end and the university name runs along the long west face. The reverse view from Dobbs shows the shorter north colonnade standing in front of that same glass slab. The fins are already visible in a 1982 archive photograph. [South approach, March 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=YyT7ZxQDZX0bRO70eXrMGA&heading=29.74&pitch=25) · [Dobbs corner, March 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=OS1__E6YNbr5ltVPMHJH9g&heading=153.95&pitch=24) · [1982 Atlanta Journal-Constitution print](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/gsu_ajc_13350)

At sidewalk height the entrance is recessed under a broad hood labeled “75 PIEDMONT.” Two piers frame the doors. The model follows the rise from sidewalk to landing, with steps, a ramp, rails and planters. Walking east on Dobbs brings the low service wing and the neighboring J Deck into view. ## Footprint and heights

The 16-vertex City footprint `4aa2b33a-6a07-4b7b-b274-59c6cb7fa8dd` spans roughly 51 × 74 m including the podium. The original extrusion applied its baked 58.3 m height across that whole outline. The tall office slab occupies only about 19 × 68 m along Piedmont. The model uses the existing site and preserves the separate 15-vertex J Deck footprint `633f3f22-6e78-4c87-8211-837a9c0fa992`. Georgia State identifies them as buildings 0083 and 0082 respectively. [Campus inventory](https://facilities.gsu.edu/files/2026/03/Building_List_03312026.pdf) · [Site aerial](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/export?bbox=-9393414.891216878,3996111.527463608,-9393292.074019609,3996234.3446608773&bboxSR=3857&imageSR=3857&size=800,800&format=jpg&f=image)

Of 6,297 USGS lidar returns inside the named footprint, the median was 354.6 m NAVD88 and the 98th percentile was 355.6 m. The adjacent Piedmont grade is roughly 302–304 m, placing the main roof about 51–52 m above the sidewalk. The east podium is near 312.3 m, the low service finger near 307 m and the entrance canopy near 307.4 m. The southwest roof well drops about 4.45 m below the main roof. The modeled edges follow the roof-return transitions and aerial image. Bay spacing, window divisions, fin depth, signage, entrance details and material colors are estimates from photographs. [Footprints over lidar elevations](lidar.webp) · [USGS Georgia Statewide Block 2, 2018](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)

A blue-hour photograph of nearby 92 Piedmont shows lit windows and light spilling onto the pavement. Individual office occupancy and crown illumination at 75 Piedmont are unresolved in that photograph. The model’s scattered lit offices are illustrative.
