# Piedmont Driving Club clubhouse

Private `studio-piedmont-driving-club` study. Model `db13de58` replaces `osm:relation:3006786` once on the `midtown-upper` row. The mapped outer shell covers about 6,490 m² and its approximately 617 m² enclosed courtyard stays open. The street arrival is beside the mapped Piedmont Avenue loop at about `(1318, -3784)`. The digital model represents the 1215 Piedmont Avenue clubhouse. [Club directions](https://drivingclub.clubhouseonline-e3.com/Directions)

The eaves are estimated at 6.85 m above entry grade and the highest main ridge at about 11.75 m. The pale courtyard gallery, rounded south projection and park-side covered terrace are drawn at low-poly scale. Terrace projection and roof positions are inferred from the photographs and aerial. The mapped drives remain the entry register. The existing courts and the larger park and pool grounds remain under the board’s existing treatment.

The frozen footprint and more recent aerial establish the current modeled extent. [1938 exterior](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Piedmont_Driving_Club_circa_1938.png)
