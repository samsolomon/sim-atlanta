# 230 Peachtree

The Peachtree Street end has three nine-window bays of exposed aggregate concrete. The middle bay projects beyond its neighbors and rises above them to screen the mechanical plant. The south wing stops short at the west end. At street level a white canopy links the glass office doors with the hotel lobby in the north bay. Portman's stainless-steel sculpture *Belle* stands in the forecourt. The hotel photograph from the opposite side of the plaza shows the white sign above the canopy, warm light through the entrance and guest windows, and illuminated strips along the water features. The district survey records removal of the original tower floodlighting.
