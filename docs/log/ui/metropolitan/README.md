# The Metropolitan, 20 Marietta Street NW

From the Five Points station plaza, Broad Street leads to the Metropolitan’s projecting **M** at Marietta. The building occupies the southeast corner. Its pale stone base turns the corner below a much darker shaft. The former Third National Bank acquired its present exterior in a mid-century refacing and later became condominiums. [Central Atlanta Progress building directory](https://www.downtownatl.com/places/the-metropolitan) · [Bull Realty offering memorandum, cover and floor plan](https://s3.amazonaws.com/realnex.production/ListingAttachment/787822/10838499.pdf)
