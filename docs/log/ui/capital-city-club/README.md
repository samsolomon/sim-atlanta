# Capital City Club

The Capital City Club (1911) stands at 7 John Portman Boulevard, formerly Harris Street, in the block behind Peachtree. It uses the Overture outline `e8dfccb6-d71d-4e0b-a58f-478c6c47c757`.

The Commons photographs show a warm tan brick club building, about four storeys above a deep one-storey porch. The porch has paired light-stone columns and a balustrade, and it runs along the boulevard front over arched ground-floor openings, with a short return toward Peachtree. The windows are punched and stone-trimmed. A stone string course and a stone cornice carry a balustraded parapet. A one-storey terrace wing lies to the west.

## Estimates

- **Body:** four window storeys at a 3.2 m bay, with a string course at 333.4.
- **Top:** a 0.6 m cornice at the eave and a 1 m balustrade.
- **Porch:** paired columns every 3.4 m along a 5.4 m-deep porch, and a short return of four columns on the Peachtree side.
- **Rear range:** one storey of windows.

**Footprint.** The porch runs unbroken across the entrance, where the mapped outline has a 5 m recess, as photograph 6100 shows. The skin declares `maxProjection: 3.2` for it. Detached parts: 0.
