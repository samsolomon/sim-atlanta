# Medical Arts parking garage

The short Alexander Street elevation and the north addition appear together in National Register photograph 5. Photograph 4 shows the original garage's enclosed side from the opposite approach. The buff brick panels, pale exposed concrete frame, dark openings and open northern slab edges distinguish the two construction campaigns. Photograph 6 shows the open deck railing and ramp. [National Register photographs and survey](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/a408edde-5c5e-4488-824b-ce1462a719f9)

The original 1927 garage has three enclosed stories. The 1954 northern addition has three covered levels and roof parking. Its top parking surface connects to the third floor of the original building. The projected plans use the three Overture part rings. The tower shares the garage edge between City (503.12, −1249.17) and (497.27, −1232.30).
