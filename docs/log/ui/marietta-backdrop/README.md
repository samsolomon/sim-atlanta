# The low Marietta frontage at Five Points

A 22.6 m stretch of Marietta between The Metropolitan and the former Fourth National Bank appeared as a third tower in City. The March 2025 street panorama shows a pale storefront and dark window openings beneath two display panels. Looking back from Five Points, the corner bank partly screens this frontage. The Metropolitan and the tall 2 Peachtree slab rise behind it as the viewer moves along Marietta. ## Footprint and elevations

The seven-vertex Overture ring begins at City (105.30, −80.24), extends 22.57 m along Marietta and runs about 35 m back to the 2 Peachtree boundary. Its stable ID is `8823b763-1d37-44f3-b2f0-8b7de989868b`. The Metropolitan touches its west side. The separate Peachtree corner record `774eb32e…` touches its east side. The study changes only the unnamed ring and makes no claim about parcel ownership or a separate street address.
