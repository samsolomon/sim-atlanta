# Omni Atlanta Hotel at Centennial Park

The architect’s park photographs show a slender north tower with a broad limestone window grid, blue-glass corner bays and a narrow light-colored end fin. The raised service block and the glazed upper strip give the roof two different profiles from the fountain and Marietta Street. The 27-story tower sits at one edge of the much lower convention podium. An enclosed bridge crosses Marietta to the older hotel at the former CNN Center. Both wings and the bridge occupy one mapped outline. [C+TC Design Studio, park views in daylight and after sunset](https://www.ctcdesignstudio.com/omni-atlanta-hotel-at-cnn-center/) · [Aerial view of the complex](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atlanta-cnn-center-aerial.jpg) · [Hotel’s north and south tower guide](https://www.omnihotels.com/-/media/images/hotels/atlcnn/meetings/pdf/atlcnn-omni-atlanta-cnn-center-group-brochure.pdf)

The Overture footprint `74fe6c27-9599-458a-bf46-94111160bd53` spans 123.5 × 166.5 m on the City axes. Its single baked height was 9 m. The 27,940 lidar returns within it separate the northern podium near 336.9 m NAVD88 from the room slab at 402.1 m, upper roof at 407.4 m and service roof near 409.2 m. The bridge roof is about 332 m. Its soffit, the glazing divisions and the width of the stone fins are photographic estimates. The old southern wing meets the adjoining office and atrium at roughly 375.4 and 382.6 m. [USGS 2018 Georgia elevation survey](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)

At dusk, blue glass reflects the open sky above the park. At night, a few warm window rows and street-level lights stand out while the piers stay comparatively dark. Room occupancy and brightness are illustrative.
