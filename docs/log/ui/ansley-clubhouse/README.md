# Ansley Golf Club clubhouse

Private model `5e2ce13a` on `study/ansley-clubhouse`, based on combined `74e0f9f0`. The clubhouse replaces the mapped `osm:way:269222144` footprint on `brookwood-station`. The mapped plan is about 2,005 m². The two street gables, open valet portico and downhill veranda put the street and golf sides at different scales. The marked valet approach follows the existing service drive near `(1484, -5155)`. [Club guest directions](https://ansleygc.org/Guest_Information.aspx)

The ridge is estimated at 10.75 m above the portico grade. The course side exposes about 6 m more height below the valet grade. Two veranda levels and a roughly 24 × 22 m lap pool are inferred from the exterior photos and imagery. The pool and its immediate apron share the board’s drawn grade. The other courts and pool structures remain generic scenery.
