# Historic Fulton County Courthouse

At Pryor and Martin Luther King Jr. Drive, six tall stone columns stand in front of a recessed wall. From the opposite street the same baked building has another identity, the dark-glass Fulton County Justice Center Tower. The live City view used one 52.8 m extrusion for both. The Charles L. Carnes Justice Center has its own 25.1 m baked building record. The Government Center across Pryor also has its own geometry. This study replaces only the combined Slaton and Justice Center Tower record. [Production before](production-before.jpg) · [Superior Court’s guide to the complex](https://fultonsuperiorcourtga.gov/directions)

**Slaton Courthouse.** The 1914 building occupies the northwest corner of the block. The National Register describes nine stories, a six-column Corinthian order spanning levels four through eight, a pedimented side pavilion and a denticulated cornice beneath the attic story. The restored facade and archival photograph guide the fluted effect, pale capital and abacus profiles, arched doors and dark window recesses. The individual column diameters, spacing, reveal depths and simplified ornament are visual estimates. [National Register architectural survey](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/1eaae4e3-ba36-498f-8194-75fbe696ff3f) · [Atlanta History Center photograph](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/geh_athpc_1663) · [Restoration team photographs](https://www.wje.com/assets/pdfs/articles/Fulton-County-Courthouse.pdf) · [Pryor Street photograph](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=JOGz1SpgQ5gxSp-IP7UlSg&heading=168&pitch=26)

**Roof and wings.** The mapped historic street front measures approximately 68 m along Pryor and 41 m along MLK. From 22,367 sampled 2018 lidar returns within the merged footprint, the historic roof is about 351–354 m NAVD88 at the edges, rising to 356–357 m along its U-shaped high roof. The modern tower has a much flatter roof near 352 m, with a narrow service block approaching 359 m. Two short links cross the intervening recess at about 347 and 350 m. Their four-metre structural depths are estimates. The east frontage uses the street photograph’s blue-black glazing and pale projecting end piers. **After dark.** The restoration cover shows warm entrance lamps on Pryor at twilight. The nighttime news photograph shows the separate justice-complex entrance and temporary incident lighting. Only the warm Pryor lamps and an illustrative scattering of occupied windows carry into the model. The sources establish no recurring facade-lighting schedule.
