# The Ritz-Carlton, Atlanta

At 181 Peachtree Street the hotel presents a short face to Peachtree and a longer one along Ellis. Its pink-brown stone bands bend around shallow projecting window bays. At the sidewalk the two sides serve different purposes. A covered dining terrace and round-headed awning face Peachtree. The carriage entrance passes under the guest rooms on Ellis. From across the street the much taller twin crowns immediately behind the hotel belong to 191 Peachtree. ## Dimensions and estimates

The named Overture record measures approximately 55 × 31 m overall. Its 94 m height and 24-floor value produced a single full-height extrusion. The USGS sample contains 4,017 returns inside that footprint. The main roof clusters near 397.0 m NAVD88, about 68 m above the Peachtree sidewalk at 329 m. The stepped rooftop housing rises to about 403.4 m NAVD88. A smaller raised unit stands farther east. The roof outline and its returns also appear in the aerial image. The recessed north section has sparse lidar coverage. Its lower volume is an estimate guided by edge returns near 358 m and the neighboring atrium. [USGS Georgia 2018 lidar](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json) · [Esri aerial](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/export?bbox=-9394007.905625263,3996352.9475425542,-9393869.435255792,3996491.417912025&bboxSR=3857&imageSR=3857&size=800,800&format=jpg&f=image)

Only the hotel footprint, ID `74911c46-8821-4b61-95d0-c571563d795d`, is consumed. The 191 Peachtree tower, its garage and the Ellis Hotel across Peachtree keep their own records. The model uses eleven folded bays on Ellis and five on Peachtree. Their number, widths, projection, floor spacing, driveway depth, terrace fittings, roof equipment and signage are visual estimates.
