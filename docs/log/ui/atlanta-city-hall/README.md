# Atlanta City Hall

From Mitchell Street, the 1930 tower rises in three steps above its pale stone base. From Trinity Avenue, the prominent roof is a glazed atrium above a curved entrance. The 1989 annex and the historic hall share one downtown building record. The live City render gave them one 66.6 m extrusion. [Production before](production-before.jpg) · [City of Atlanta preservation survey](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/historic-preservation/property-district-information/atlanta-city-hall)

The Historic American Buildings Survey gives the original base as 160 × 116 ft (48.8 × 35.4 m) and the tower as 82 × 54 ft (25.0 × 16.5 m). In City the mapped base is about 46.9 × 36.4 m. The marble porch, olive spandrels and pale terra-cotta piers follow the survey, archival corner photographs and the present Mitchell approach. The width of each bay and the depth of its relief are visual estimates. [HABS GA-2166, physical description](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/ga/ga0400/ga0460/data/ga0460data.pdf) · [Archive, Mitchell and Washington](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/geh_athpc_1189) · [Second archival angle](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/geh_athpc_2314) · [Mitchell Street approach](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=92horWigUSswyX_KaL5iUg&heading=215&pitch=29)

The 2018 lidar sample contains 27,284 returns at the site. Approximate roof elevations in metres NAVD88 are 319 at the link, 327 at the historic base, 356.5, 364.7 and 371.7 at the tower setbacks, and 376.2 at its peak. Across the link, the annex steps from about 327 to 334 m with glazed ridges near 337 m. The aerial and Trinity frontage guide the atrium, end wings and curved vestibule. Ridge pitches, vestibule radius and individual pane sizes are estimates. [USGS 2018 lidar](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json) · [Trinity Avenue approach](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=Udb93oddliiMGabkZ_Y3tQ&heading=19&pitch=16) · [Esri aerial](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/export?bbox=-9394407.06151639,3995004.968598013,-9394224.039810654,3995187.990303748&bboxSR=3857&imageSR=3857&size=800,800&format=jpg&f=image)

The city’s night photograph shows alternating amber and cream light along the piers and dark glass between them. City uses one illustrative lighting arrangement from that programmable installation.
