# 100 Edgewood Avenue

The Hartford Building opened in 1965 on the north side of Hurt Park. It later housed United Way as the Robert C. Woodruff Volunteer Service Center. Georgia State’s acquisition record describes a concrete-framed tower at 100 Edgewood Avenue with a basement and 17 stories above grade. Its building ring in City measures about 58.4 m along Edgewood and 34–35 m from the park frontage to the rear. The parking deck immediately north and the Loudermilk United Way Building farther northeast have separate footprints. [Georgia preservation survey](https://dca.georgia.gov/document/document/modern-downtown-atlanta/download) · [Board of Regents acquisition record](https://www.usg.edu/regents/assets/regents/documents/board_meetings/BoR_August_8_2023_-_Agenda_%28Public%29_revised_8-7-23.pdf) · [Production City before](production-before.jpg)

This study follows the exterior in Georgia State’s photograph. The university’s November 4, 2026 update gives February 2027 as the anticipated start for facade renovations. [Georgia State project update](https://operations.gsu.edu/college-town-downtown/) · [University photograph of the tower and entrance](https://operations.gsu.edu/files/2025/10/100_edgewood.png)

## Roof and terrain

The named Overture ring is `f56ffe3c-cf56-4bc0-9ed3-eefadc1d7e89`. Its earlier extrusion assigned 66.6 m to the whole tower. The source comparison uses 5,015 sampled lidar returns inside this ring and the City terrain grid. All elevations below are metres NAVD88. [USGS Georgia statewide point cloud](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)

| Surface | Elevation | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground around the tower | Southwest 309.7, southeast 307.5, northeast 309.1, northwest 311.5 | The southeast corner is about 2.2 m lower than the southwest. The park entrance stair meets a level terrace. |
| Main roof and outer parapet | 376.3 and 377.5–377.6 | Roof deck about 65–69 m above the ground at the four corners. |
| Long roof housing and low covers | 381.7–381.9 and about 382.5 | The housing stands about 5.5 m above the main roof. The three ridged covers add approximately 0.7 m. |
| Western equipment well | Floor about 374.8, perimeter screen near 381.7, fan tops near 381.5 | The well floor sits about 1.5 m below the main roof. The two fans sit below the top of their screen. |
| Volunteer Center parking deck and Loudermilk United Way | Deck near 325.8, main Loudermilk roof near 329.3 | Both are independently recorded and substantially lower than the office tower. |

The photographed bay arrangement, rooftop plan and lidar elevations guide the model. The depth of the window recesses, dimensions of the frame and piers, door positions, stair risers, rails, window coverings, equipment slats and night occupancy are visual estimates.
