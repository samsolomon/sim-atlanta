# Rhodes-Haverty Building

At 134 Peachtree Street the road bends past three decorated faces of the 1929 Rhodes-Haverty Building, now the Residence Inn Atlanta Downtown. From the north, a fourth, much plainer brick wall carries a tall illuminated advertisement. The former City extrusion concealed most of this face behind a second tower. [Production City before](production-before.jpg) · [Atlanta landmark designation](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/historic-preservation/property-district-information/rhodes-haverty-building)

## Sidewalk, roof and sign dimensions

The nomination gives property-line lengths of 82.85 ft on Peachtree, 95.78 ft on Williams, 72.77 ft on Forsyth and 62.3 ft along the north wall. These convert to 25.25, 29.19, 22.18 and 18.99 m. The model follows the four corners of the baked building ring. The 21-story count is documented. [National Register boundary description](https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/79000725.pdf)
