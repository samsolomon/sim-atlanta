# Georgia Aquarium at Pemberton Place

From Centennial Olympic Park, the aquarium has two bows. The high blue-glass lantern belongs to the original 2005 building. The lower blue-and-silver wall curves around the entrance that opened on October 23, 2020. From Luckie Street, the same complex looks quite different: a high blue hall, a tall window and a row of portholes at sidewalk level. Georgia Aquarium gives its street address as 225 Baker Street NW and lists the parking deck separately at 357 Luckie Street NW. [Aquarium expansion chronology](https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/expansion-2020/) · [Aquarium visitor directions](https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/resource-center/deep-dive/how-to-visit-georgia-aquarium/) · [Original cladding supplier’s architectural account](https://www.alucobondusa.com/blog/georgia-aquarium/)

## Why this footprint

The Georgia World Congress Center already has its twelve numbered exhibit halls, the BC connector, its entrances, rail crossing and courtyards in City. Its 597 m footprint sits on the downtown board, with about 11 m to spare at its western edge. AmericasMart, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, State Farm Arena, City Hall and the Fulton County Government Center also have individual models. The Boisfeuillet Jones Atlanta Civic Center on Piedmont belongs to the north extension, where the redevelopment combines the performing arts center, exhibition hall and newer construction. [GWCC study and floor plans](../../../georgia-world-congress-center.md) · [Atlanta Housing site plan](https://www.atlantahousing.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2022-0021_AHA-CivicCenter_RFQ-Final-08-31-21..pdf)

I ranked the remaining named downtown records by exterior footprint area and checked the recognizable street approaches. Areas below are rounded ground footprints from the bake. Northern skyline assignments include Peachtree Summit and the Peachtree Center towers. The aquarium is fully inside the downtown board and has a public silhouette visible from both the park and Pemberton Place. [Northern skyline shortlist](../55-marietta/README.md)

## The elevations in photographs

- **Pemberton Place.** A silver rim caps the darker blue fascia. The spaced name sits above a wave of small reflective plates. Around the curve, a ribbon of glass and the recessed entrance sit under the projecting roof. The actual cladding manufacturer describes a wind-responsive kinetic wall. City uses a fixed pattern of highlights. [September 2024 approach](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7623446,-84.3939256&heading=314.27&pitch=14&fov=88&source=outdoor) · [Josh Green’s completed entrance photograph](https://atlanta.urbanize.city/sites/default/files/field/image/Front%20Lead%3F.jpg) · [EXTECH façade study](https://extechinc.com/case-studies/ga-aquarium-kineticwall/)
- **The lantern.** The aquarium’s oblique aerial shows the older glass prow, its fish-shaped G and projecting blade above the newer entrance. The same emblem and the lettering are visible at a much larger scale on the broad western roof in county imagery. [Aquarium’s aerial photograph](https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/DJI_0141.jpg) · [Fulton County 2024 orthophoto](https://gismaps.fultoncountyga.gov/arcgispub2/rest/services/Imagery/Pictometry2024_NAIP/MapServer/export?bbox=-9394959.738904102,3996909.83924586,-9394646.675460082,3997222.90268988&bboxSR=3857&imageSR=3857&size=1200,1200&format=jpg&f=image)
- **Luckie and Baker.** The April 2026 street photographs show the portholes below a tall curved window, then the blue street corner with a digital display high on the wall. The sign wraps the corner above a gray masonry base. The screen illustration in City is a still image drawn for the model. [Luckie Street window](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7631630,-84.3961321&heading=97.02&pitch=11&fov=88) · [Baker corner](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7623266,-84.3953750&heading=72.53&pitch=15&fov=88&source=outdoor) · [Photograph of the operating screen](https://atlanta.urbanize.city/post/photos-how-georgia-aquariums-100m-redo-sharks-exhibit-turned-out)

The 2020 aerial and subsequent street photographs identify which elements survived and where the new fascia and display stand. The present-day intensities, current sign colors and screen content in City are illustrative. ## Dimensions and estimates

The 47-vertex Overture footprint `bb792e0e-9fe2-4c92-8ee9-dd3c60fa4dbb` spans about **184 × 214 m**. The L-shaped, 7,165 m² garage `40215321-bea4-48dc-a8b7-5a05a7206f36` remains a separate record at its northwest corner. The contractor reports **104,149 ft² across four stories** for the 2020 addition. That is floor area. [Contractor’s project dimensions](https://www.brasfieldgorrie.com/projects/the-georgia-aquarium/) · [Point elevations and footprint boundaries](lidar.webp)

| Part of building | Evidence and modeled elevation |
| --- | --- |
| Luckie hall | The 2018 returns place the roof near 331–332 m NAVD88, about 22–23 m above the west sidewalk. |
| Broad main roof | Returns descend from roughly 324 m to 318–319 m toward Baker. The aerial locates the equipment and skylight groups, painted roof emblem and wordmark. |
| North roof terraces | The lower and upper plates measure about 318.1 and 323.3 m. Narrow strips between the larger roofs reach about 318 m. |
| Glass prow | Roof surface near 326.7 m, with higher returns along the edge near 331–332 m. The slender blade and mullion spacing are photo estimates. |
| Curved entrance and screen | The **2018 survey predates the 2020 entrance**. The county aerial constrains its plan. Eave heights of roughly 318–323 m, the recessed doors, cant of the wall, sign dimensions and the Baker screen height are photo estimates. |

There were 64,028 sampled returns inside the modern aquarium outline and 17,934 inside the garage outline. The former plaza contributes low returns to the aquarium sample. Applying those low heights to the current entrance would remove a wing now visible from across the park.
