# World of Coca-Cola from Pemberton Place

From Baker Street, the Georgia Aquarium's blue bow frames the left of Pemberton Place. A green bottle suspended inside a narrow glass tower marks the museum on the right. At the ticket entrance, the tower passes through a sweeping silver canopy. The canopy falls toward Baker, exposing different parts of the etched script as you walk along the glass. The museum opened here in May 2007. [Owner's history](https://www.worldofcoca-cola.com/about-us) · [Both buildings in City](both-day-after.webp)

## What locates the model

The downtown bake represents the museum with a single 12.12 m extrusion. Its 66-vertex Overture footprint, `238d116b-be0d-4143-8430-7dce06763b88`, encloses **6,560 m²** and spans **109.8 m east–west by 95.9 m north–south**. The folded north boundary runs into the open service court. Two adjacent small records at the northwest and Baker entrances have their own outlines. City replaces only the museum record. [Footprint and lidar](lidar.webp) · [Roof and court in City](roof-day-after.webp)

| Lighting | The designer's paired photographs show a sea-green bottle in a cool glass enclosure and a warm orange lobby behind dark mullions. The green and amber are a fixed illustrative lighting state. Brightness, coverage and the rear poster drawing are estimates. |

The plotted survey contains **14,461 sampled returns** inside the baked exterior, using levels 7–10 of the 2018 USGS point cloud. The older design photographs include a reflecting pool. The 2024 county image shows lawn, planting and a curving paved walk in front of the museum. City follows that later forecourt.
