# The Center, formerly CNN Center

From Centennial Olympic Park the office walls form a grid of concrete piers. From the arena side, the complex climbs out of the Gulch in a series of diagonal terraces. An aerial photograph shows the darker atrium roof rising behind the offices, with angled clerestory glass beneath its edge. The hotel adjoins the office walls and crosses Marietta on a separate mapped outline. The model follows the standing structure. The leasing materials also show proposed redevelopment. [Owner’s street and park photographs](https://cpgcre.com/project/cnn-center/) · [Aerial exterior](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atlanta-cnn-center-aerial.jpg) · [Owner’s building history](https://www.thecenteratlanta.com/about/)

The 35-vertex Overture footprint `20a8be94-7717-42fd-a6e7-75c22214f4aa` spans 149.1 × 157.7 m on the City axes. It arrives with one 67.4 m height. Of the 30,187 lidar returns inside it, the main office plates cluster around 375.4 m NAVD88 and the central atrium reaches 382.6 m. The western terraces rise roughly 4.6 m per level from about 341 m. Mullion spacing, the glass slope, materials and the arrangement of rooftop equipment are photo-scaled estimates. [USGS Georgia 2018 elevation data](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)

The fountain-side night photograph shows light through a scattering of windows and bright street lighting below. The concrete dividers stay comparatively dark. The modeled warm windows represent an illustrative occupancy pattern. The old CNN sign visible in the historical photographs was specific to that period.
