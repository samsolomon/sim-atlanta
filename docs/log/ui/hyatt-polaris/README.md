# Hyatt Regency Atlanta and Polaris

From Baker Street the blue dome rises on a narrow pedestal above the broad atrium roof. An aerial photograph from the southwest shows the paler International Tower and the bronze cylindrical Radius Tower in front. After dark the dome’s glowing horizontal bands are visible above the hotel. The historic district survey dates the original hotel to 1967, the Ivy/Radius addition to 1971 and the International Tower to 1982. [Daytime aerial](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hyatt-regency-atlanta-aerial.jpg) · [Night photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/doctorcasino/47618702881/) · [1975 Polaris close-up](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/geh_jilson_273) · [Historic district survey, p. 67](https://georgiawildlife.com/sites/default/files/hpd/pdf/Peachtree%20Center%20HD%20Final%204-24-17.pdf)

One Overture footprint (`0feb6547-009b-487f-954b-3a4f2a864df6`) carried an 8 m extrusion across all three towers. The study uses 50,738 lidar returns inside that outline. City x runs east and z south. Elevations below are metres NAVD88. [USGS 2018 Georgia lidar](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)
