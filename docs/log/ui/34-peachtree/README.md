# 34 Peachtree at Woodruff Park

From across the park, 34 Peachtree has pale blue-gray metal strips between its darker window rows, three stronger vertical mullions across the Peachtree elevation and a pale, flat crown. Turn onto the Walton side and a full-height, opaque bronze-gray strip splits the glazing. The tower rises next to the older William-Oliver Building. Peachtree and the streetcar corridor give a clear view toward the crown above the trees, while the Five Points station approach puts the neighboring buildings in front of much of the shaft. [Tom Caiafa’s 2007 eastern view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:34_Peachtree.JPG) · [Woodruff Park street photograph](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=rZey7nHk3PdAD7EaoHz4eg&heading=230&pitch=26&fov=88) · [Peachtree streetcar approach](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=33.7558965,-84.3888418&heading=216&pitch=22&fov=88)

Hedrick and Stanley designed the 1961 National Bank of Georgia Building with an aluminum-and-glass curtain wall, shops at grade and a banking lobby one floor up. The old rooftop bank sign in the archive photograph has gone. The blue **34 PEACHTREE** lettering above the street entrance is visible in Fulton County’s 2017 shuttle guide. W2’s aerial photograph and floor plan establish the block and the two street frontages. The facade bay counts, finishes and letter spacing are photo estimates. [State historic context, Bank of Georgia entry](https://dca.georgia.gov/document/resources/modern-downtown-atlanta-multiple-properties-form/download) · [Fulton County, p. 7](https://fultoncountyga.gov/-/media/Departments/Human-Resources-Management/New-Employee-Information/Fulton_County_Shuttle_Customer_Manual_August_2017.pdf#page=7) · [W2 floor plan and project sheet](https://w2rep.com/portfolio_page/34-peachtree-atlanta-georgia/)
