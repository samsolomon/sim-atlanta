# Piedmont Avenue gates at 12th and 14th Streets

The park's two rough-granite entrances on Piedmont Avenue are drawn at the OSM gate nodes `node/316638596` (12th Street) and `node/5674178517` (14th Street). SAH Archipedia credits the stone gates at the 10th, 12th and 14th Street entrances to Edwards and Sayward in the 1920s. The NPS nomination puts the 14th Street gate around the 1911 Peace Monument, so that date is unconfirmed. Smith Dalia added the iron gates at 14th in 1996. The 10th Street entrance lies on the board's south row edge and is outside this change.

## References

Each was opened in a fresh Orca tab.

- **14th Street, day.** [Wikimedia: Main Entrance to Piedmont Park](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Main_Entrance_to_Piedmont_Park_Atlanta.jpg) (2013, straight on from across Piedmont Ave). It shows two tall rough-granite piers with stepped pyramid caps and finials, black steel posts and iron leaves, and low granite walls sweeping out to smaller end piers. More views: [Peace Monument category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Peace_Monument_(Atlanta)).
- **12th Street, day.** [Flickr 5743665419](https://www.flickr.com/photo.gne?id=5743665419) (looking in through the open gates, opened in Orca). [Flickr 5743663459](https://www.flickr.com/photo.gne?id=5743663459) (pier and leaf) and the sign-beam view below came from the research pass, and I did not open them myself. Together they show shorter piers with flat slab caps and bronze plaques, double black iron leaves, and the cut-out "PIEDMONT PARK" beam ([Flickr 3771563017](https://www.flickr.com/photo.gne?id=3771563017)).
- **Night.** No reliable night photograph of either gate exists, which is a gap. Neither gate has a fixture on its piers in the day photographs. The research pass describes black acorn path lamps lining the path just inside 12th Street. The lamp style comes from a [Conservancy photo](https://piedmontpark.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dockside-Front.jpg), and the warm amber glow from the lake bridge's night photographs. Their count and positions are estimates.
- **Boundary wall.** I found no source for a continuous wall along Piedmont Avenue. The photographs show low walls only near each gate, with a sidewalk and hedge between the gates. Only the gate walls are drawn.
