# Marquis One

The 1985 office tower at 245 Peachtree Center Avenue uses the mapped Overture outline `d36a5d7e-165d-4008-a4be-6d32c4c55f0b` (about 57 × 32 m, 115.2 m high). The light precast panels and deep glazing read as a narrow vertical grid in the leasing photograph. The archival hotel-side angle shows the shallow breaks between the shaft bays. The district nomination describes the double-height piers, stair entrance on Peachtree Center Avenue and surviving barrel hood on Harris Street. [Leasing exterior](https://peachtreecre.com/property/marquis-one/) · [1990 hotel-side angle](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/gsu_ajc_8373) · [District nomination, pp. 44 and 46](https://saportareport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/Peachtree-Center-HD-Final-4-24-17.pdf#page=46)

The model starts from the mapped height and assigns 11.6 m to the entrance and underside, about 3.9 m to each of 26 window courses and 1.5 m to the parapet. The estimated pane pitch is 1.55 m and the recess is stylised at 0.12 m. The equipment screen, stair and entry dimensions are estimates. The night skyline photograph shows scattered warm and white office windows around Peachtree Center, but the hotel and foreground obscure a reliable read of this tower's crown and lobby. City uses illustrative, stable warm interior occupancy. [Night skyline photograph](https://explorecams.com/photos/WwAPDZzyzW?lens=sony-fe-35mm-f1-4-za)
