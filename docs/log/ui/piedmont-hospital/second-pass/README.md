# Piedmont Atlanta Hospital frontage study

Private North r16 refinement of `14de5757`. The short Collier wall at the southeast tip now has an estimated 9.4 m glazed return and a 12.3 m terracotta bay, measured along the mapped face. The glass wraps onto Peachtree and rises about 5.2 m higher than the clay bay. The ground-floor glass sets back 1.05 m along the court and up to 2.8 m at the corner below the timber soffit. This split comes from the corner shown in the July 2022 Street View at 3 Collier and the submitted October 2025 Peachtree view. The angle changes the apparent proportions. The two photos and HKS’s oblique exterior show where the two materials meet and how they turn at the prow. The dimensions are visual estimates.
