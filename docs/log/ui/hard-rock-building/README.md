# Hard Rock Cafe building, 215 Peachtree Street

This is the six-storey stone commercial block at Peachtree Street and Andrew Young International Boulevard, home of the Hard Rock Cafe since November 1992. It replaces the Overture outline `2734ea6e-3114-4acb-a02f-06ff16fe76d5`, about 55 × 32 m.

**Height.** The Overture height of 69.3 m is wrong. The 22 April 2019 lidar measures the roof at p50 22.3 m, p95 26.3 m and max 30.1 m above grade, and the maximum is the rooftop sign. The model uses:
- a 5.2 m storefront storey;
- five upper floors at 3.5 m, to 22.7 m;
- a cornice to 23.5 m and a parapet to 24.2 m;
- rooftop plant to 26.8 m.

**Left off.** The neon guitar, the rooftop "Hard Rock" sign and all wordmarks are logos, so none are drawn. Night light comes only from the storefront and windows.

## Estimates

- Bay rhythm: paired windows, 2.65 m apart, between piers every 5.3 m.
- Awnings: one per bay on the Peachtree (west) and International (south) faces, with a few bays left bare.
- Rooftop plant: an 8 × 6 m box, position estimated.
- Stone `#d6cdbd`. Window glass `#5a6266` (S15 V40), recessed punched windows.

## Lighting

At night the storefront glazing is lit on the two street faces, through the lamp mesh. About 8% of the upper windows are lit, from skin-kit `litFacade` (offices above a restaurant).
