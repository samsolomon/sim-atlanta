# Empire Building at Broad and Marietta

The J. Mack Robinson College building has eleven floors of paired windows above a three-story limestone banking base. Broad Street has a central group of three rusticated arches. The door occupies the southern one, under a pediment. Shallow niches and large rectangular windows flank the arches. At the Marietta end the entrance sits furthest from Broad. The preservation survey distinguishes the Walton Street arrangement at the other end. Daytime views of the short elevation, shaft and close entrance show buff masonry, dark window frames, pale coursed stone, and horizontal trim at the top floors. [National Register nomination, description](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/fb812e1b-f37e-436a-a1b7-78fdb1c066e2) · [Street corner and two entrance angles, SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/GA-01-121-0042)

The former bank runs 62.5 m down Broad and 24.4 m down Marietta. The dimensions match the 204 and 80 feet reported by SAH. Overture gives 14 floors and leaves height blank. Bare earth is near 320–321 m NAVD88. The 2018 lidar places the main roof near 375.1 m and a narrow mechanical enclosure near 384 m. On the east side the beam returns inside two light courts drop to about 345.0 m and 334.7 m. Their cutouts are visible from above. Bay widths, stone courses, pigments, ornament and the outlines of mechanical housings are illustrative. [USGS Georgia Statewide B2 2018 point cloud](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json) · [Earlier Commerce and Robinson roof comparison](../commerce-building/README.md)

## Broad Street pediments (November 23)

The coordinator asked whether the pediments sat where the real ones do. They didn't. SAH Archipedia's fourth image shows the three rusticated arches up close. In each arch a pediment about three quarters of the span crowns a rectangular opening with an entablature. It sits wholly below an impost band at the springline, and the lunette rises above the band. The model had drawn full-span pediments straddling the springline. They now span 0.75 of the arch and sit on an entablature at 326.4 m, with the apex 0.22 m below the band. The window arches gain rusticated jambs, so the opening narrows to match the door's aedicule. [SAH image 4](https://archipedia-images.s3.amazonaws.com/360/GA-01-121-0042_004.jpg)

The photograph also shows one lunette filled with stone and one arched aedicule with a finial. Their positions on this elevation couldn't be confirmed, so they aren't modelled.
