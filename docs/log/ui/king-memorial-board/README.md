# Crossing from downtown to King Memorial

City’s opt-in east board covers x 1000–1800 m and z 280–640 m. The first 800 m reaches the station site and crossover. King Memorial is the first train destination. Subsequent sections should fill neighboring streets on the same lattice so the wider board grows toward a roughly square footprint. [Corridor study, measurements and rail limits](../../../king-memorial-corridor.md)

## Reproduce the crossing

1. Start with the usual downtown City data. Run `node scripts/stage-corridor.mjs` and `pnpm dev`.
2. Open `/?view=city&east=king-memorial&q=high&t=2026-11-20T10:15:00-05:00&speed=0`. Pan east along Decatur Street from the Connector. For the matched camera use `__city.lookAt(800,452,{bearing:78,metresPerPixel:.76,tilt:52})` in the dev build.
3. Pan toward x 954, z 452, then across x 1000. Return to the first camera for the second image. Continue to x 1490, z 445 for the station block or x 1700, z 460 for the crossover. Repeat with `q=low` for the phone rendering tier.

## What stands on the first block

**Marchon, 240 Grant Street SE.** MARTA describes 305 apartments plus office and retail space on its former overflow parking lot south of the guideway. The street entrance to the station is on the Decatur side. The Grant Street tunnel connects to the development. The USGS bare-earth survey spans late 2018 to April 2019, before the apartments opened. It supplies ground under today’s neighborhood. [MARTA opening and access](https://itsmarta.com/marta-celebrates-opening-king-memorial-tod.aspx) · [Marchon property address and gallery](https://livemarchon.com/gallery/) · [Anne Michael Sustman’s 2022 architectural photographs](https://www.anne-michael.com/marchon.html)

The October 8, 2023 USDA NAIP aerial shows a bent main apartment block enclosing a courtyard, a separate parking structure along the rail side and two long wings to the south. The February 2024 OSM mapping puts the main mass in relation 17262953, whose outer contour is split across two open ways, and the southern wings in ways 1254383435 and 1254383436. The main mass is tagged five levels and the two wings four each. The November 2025 Grant Street panorama shows dark shop glazing below four rows of pale panels, paired windows and recessed balconies. The first east-board preview illustrated this verified face. Bay widths, recess depths and the usual 3.6 m per floor are estimates. The 2022 architectural photographs show warm lighting under the courtyard walkways at dusk. The Grant Street storefront hours and individual apartment lights are unknown. City gives a sparse fixed selection of sashes and shop panes warm interiors after dark. The garage footprint is mapped; its height and internal floors remain unresolved. ### Marchon from Oakland Avenue

The east side has a different ground floor. In the March 2025 street photograph from 248 Oakland Avenue SE, the nearest four-story wing has paired windows, dark masonry at sidewalk level and a dark vertical band. Farther down Oakland the larger block has projecting and recessed bays. The 2022 project photographs show balcony stacks looking into the courtyard, and the architect describes parking along the rail side. [Oakland Avenue street photograph](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=hKtqwl2fnrKyXt3xibnCog&heading=304&pitch=9&fov=88) · [Scott Chester’s courtyard photograph](https://www.anne-michael.com/uploads/1/3/7/9/137921944/marchon-2022-0009-425x283.jpg) · [Architect’s project record and photographs](https://www.anne-michael.com/marchon.html)

City selects one reusable apartment facade for the identified five-story main block and the two four-story residential wings. Palette and bay layout are independent. The Grant frontage has street-floor glazing. The Oakland wing end has a window-led elevation. Courtyard returns mix paired windows, residential entries and stacked balcony recesses. Walls adjoining the garage have plain service bases. The garage occupies its own mapped footprint with an illustrative height and a plain concrete finish. Individual door locations, occupancy, tints and the sequence of bays remain illustrative.

## Staging, cost and ownership

The committed context is reproducible with `.venv/bin/python pipeline/scripts/king_memorial_context.py [cached-osm-map.json]` using Shapely and the corridor script’s Python environment. The source bbox and SHA-256 of the input are embedded in `context.json`. With the same raw snapshot the bake is deterministic. The stage command copies it and the merged corridor assets into Vite’s static data tree. Run the stage command before rebuilding the web bundle to publish this opt-in preview.

Desktop Orca’s resource timeline showed seven downtown scene assets and zero east data requests at downtown opening on both high and forced-low quality. Approaching the join fetched three static east assets once. The manifest is 1,743 bytes, the elevation grid 23,506 bytes and neighborhood context 61,714 bytes, totaling 86,963 bytes raw or 24,219 bytes when compressed individually with gzip level 9. A separate City code chunk for constructing the extension also loads on approach. Traversing to the station and crossover did not fetch them again. The separate evidence pack `corridor.json` belongs to the research preview. In ordinary City the camera stopped at x 1000 even after panning directly toward the station. With the east context missing, the flagged City also kept the camera downtown.

Rail can attach its station, piers, guideway and train visibility after `loadEast` validates and the east area mounts in City. Use the returned area’s `groundAt(x,z)` for the drawn ground level on that quality tier. The rail profile owner supplies the absolute structure or vehicle heights. The downtown traffic and vehicle clipping planes remain scoped to downtown. The evidence screenshots above show the board checkpoint; the combined train-arrival capture is pending Rail’s scheduled fixture and attachment.

The context also carries 49 crown centers and approximate radii traced on the October 2023 NAIP image. The 800 × 360 pixel export matches the strip at one pixel per scene metre, north up. Most of the distinct large crowns are north of the rail line or in Oakland Cemetery. Heights and seasonal colors are illustrative. Dense or ambiguous canopy and newer plantings are incomplete.
