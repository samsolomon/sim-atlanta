# The three buildings of AmericasMart

Three supported Overture building outlines occupy the blocks around Ted Turner Drive and John Portman Boulevard. The old City view gave each market a single roof elevation. Narrow building polygons also filled the space between the sidewalk and several bridge decks. The street camera below looks north from Andrew Young International Boulevard. The fork at the next intersection connects the Merchandise and Apparel marts diagonally. The 2018 USGS survey yielded 30,149 points in Building 1, 52,412 in Building 2 and 25,294 in Building 3 in the sampled tiles. Heights above are representative roof clusters. Bridge roofs along Ted Turner range from roughly 329 to 388 m NAVD88. Around scene z = −731, successive returns fall near 347, 357, 367 and 388 m on almost the same plan line. Across Portman, five distinguishable alignments and the diagonal fork have returns. The district nomination counts additional connections, including stacked passages that an overhead survey may conceal. The model depicts supported alignments, with estimated enclosure depths, mullion pitch and stair apertures. One additional mapped Ted Turner span near z = −682 has no midspan roof return in the sampled 2018 points. Its 343.5 m top is illustrative. Only the matching generic bridge footprints and double-drawn pedestrian strips are replaced.
