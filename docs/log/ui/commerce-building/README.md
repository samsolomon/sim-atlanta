# Bennett A. Brown Commerce Building

The pale tower at 34 Broad Street NW stands on the northwest corner of Broad and Marietta. Georgia State identifies its Bennett Brown Room on the 18th floor. The state preservation survey identifies the same property as Tucker and Howell’s 1960 Chamber of Commerce Building, with street-level retail and nine floors of parking. The named City ring follows the two street fronts visible in the September 1960 Lane Brothers photograph. The 17-floor, 66.6 m extrusion had window bands through the entire garage. [Georgia State University](https://events.gsu.edu/explore-event-spaces/) · [Georgia preservation survey, printed pp. 31–32](https://dca.georgia.gov/document/document/modern-downtown-atlanta/download#page=37) · [Production City before](production-before.jpg)

## Roof and neighbors

The Overture ring `c4d0fd71-eba0-46b2-b551-1e840e220b6e` has about 23.5 m of Marietta frontage and 32.2 m of Broad frontage. There are 2,532 sampled returns inside it from the 2018 Georgia lidar. Ground and roof values below use metres NAVD88. The overhead image helps distinguish the rooftop plant from the surrounding buildings. Its oblique displacement makes it unsuitable for locating the walls at grade. Only the Commerce ring is replaced. The garage mouth, facade finishes, vent depths and pitch, grille weave, upper openings, simplified relief sculpture and small rooftop details are estimates from photographs. The published 18-story count includes levels whose floor lines are concealed behind the parking screens.
