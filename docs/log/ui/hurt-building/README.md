# The Hurt Building from Woodruff Park and Hurt Park

From Woodruff Park, the entrance appears almost round beneath the narrow end of the tower. Walk east along Edgewood and the opposite end resolves into two wings, with daylight between them above the lower floors. The baked Overture footprint (`a7e88f9b-fa39-42c0-9804-9117c9cf8a7c`) gave the whole triangle, including the rotunda, one height of 82 m. The City model now distinguishes the marble base, projecting entrance drum, paired sash, buff brick wings and bracketed cornice. Hurt Plaza Garage retains its own footprint. [Atlanta preservation survey](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/historic-preservation/property-district-information/hurt-building) · [National Register nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/50364b0b-c806-483b-9989-4825c49a112e/)

The western sunset photograph shows four main window openings across the face above the entrance, including the closer central pair. The photograph from Hurt Park shows the court opening behind an uninterrupted lower street facade. A roof aerial helped distinguish the mechanical block from the parapets.
