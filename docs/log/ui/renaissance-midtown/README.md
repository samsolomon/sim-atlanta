# Renaissance Atlanta Midtown Hotel

The Renaissance Atlanta Midtown Hotel, at 866 West Peachtree Street, replaces the mapped outline `osm:way:234888045`, a 56 × 18 m slab on an east–west axis. The mapped height of 64 m stands. The 22 April 2019 lidar measures the roof at p50 61.5 m, p95 66.5 m and max 67.9 m above grade (ground 293.2 m NAVD88).

The model has:
- a 9 m dark podium;
- 18 room floors at 3 m to 63 m;
- a parapet at 64 m;
- rooftop plant at 66.5 m.

## Estimates

- Bays: windows at about 3.2 m on the long faces, two columns either side of the slot on the ends. The facade is laid out on the slab's four true faces, not the OSM ring's split segments.
- The slot is recessed in life and drawn flush, as a colour band with its own panes.
- Podium height and canopy depth.
- Plant box size and position.

## Glass

The window glass is `#8a9499` (S10 V60) and the slot `#8d9ba4`. At 11:30, high tier, the sunlit south face renders glass at a median H156 S6 V53, against S12 V55 for generic office glass in the same frame. The shaded east end renders S21 V35 (advisory).

## Lighting

There is no full-night exterior photograph, so that gap is recorded here. As a hotel it is noticeably lit: about 30% of rooms, from skin-kit `litFacade`, including the slot. The podium storefront is lit and the crown is dark.
