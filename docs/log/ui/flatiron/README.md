# Atlanta’s Flatiron from Woodruff Park

The 1897 English-American Building fills the wedge between Broad, Peachtree and Poplar. At the north end a single window fits across the narrow face. Walk south along Peachtree and the other end rolls around the corner onto Poplar. The existing City outline captured both ends. Its blue ribbon glass obscured the projecting bays and double-height entrance visible across Woodruff Park. [City of Atlanta landmark designation](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/historic-preservation/property-district-information/flatiron-building) · [Woodruff Park photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atlanta%E2%80%99s_Flatiron_Building_viewed_from_Woodruff_Park.jpg) · [Existing City view](facade-before.jpg)

## What the photographs support

- At Peachtree and Luckie the single-file windows and overhanging cornice give the north tip its scale. From across Peachtree, five canted window bays stand forward of the flat end bays. Tall engaged columns flank the storefront entrance, with a round window above the doors. Both long elevations carry projecting bays. [March 2025, 2 Luckie](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=g2TCqR19Jl1n2nDAkQqJuw&heading=212.47&pitch=25&fov=88) · [March 2025, 77 Peachtree](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=d0zL4yhYhgkAde4_b5XFTA&heading=329.42&pitch=25&fov=88) · [Edge-on photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flatiron_Building_%28Atlanta%29_edge-on_from_the_corner_of_Peachtree_and_Luckie_Streets.jpg) · [September 2012 elevation](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atlanta_English-American_Building_2012_09_15_01_6139.JPG)
- The National Register describes an eleven-story building with a two-story limestone base, seven-story shaft and two-story cap. Its description of curved corner glass applies to Peachtree and Poplar. The sharp Broad–Peachtree end has a short, flat face. [National Register nomination, description p. 2](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/bf869059-2885-4fba-8b18-906de121b4d4#page=2) · [South corner from the park](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atlanta%E2%80%99s_Flatiron_Building_viewed_from_Woodruff_Park.jpg)
- The pale roof has a raised interior outline and a small service area. City simplifies those outlines within the original lot. [Esri overhead, centered on the roof](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?basemap=satellite&center=-84.3889,33.7559&level=20)

## Dimensions

The existing `FlatironCity` footprint (`200f8a75-44d8-4a11-8ff8-6af148cc6b17`) has 12 vertices. Its two long edges measure 39.61 and 38.22 m. The narrow face measures 4.37 m and the straight Poplar edge measures 18.03 m before the rounded corner. The City bake gives 44 m and 11 floors.

The terrain grid around the perimeter ranges from **320.5 to 321.5 m NAVD88**. Among 1,924 2018 lidar returns inside the footprint, the densest half-metre bin starts at 364.5 m. The median is 364.8 m and the 90th percentile is 366.2 m. This sample includes facade and roof fittings. The main roof sits 44 m above the lowest perimeter sample. The raised interior roof and service enclosure reach 0.88 and 1.65 m above that level. The model fits the lower cornice at 8.9 m and the upper cornice at 35.8 m. Those elevations, the 0.72 m projection of the shaft bays, sash spacing and portal dimensions are estimates from photographs. ## After dark

Central Atlanta Progress’s 2017 blue-hour view from the park places the Flatiron at the left. Street lamps light the foot of the building, while most upper windows read dark. The building owner’s photograph from the October 2018 *Party With the Past* event shows the warm entrance through the trees. The Lane Brothers’ 1959 night photograph supplies historical context. These images don’t establish a present-day permanent facade-lighting scheme or the occupancy of individual rooms. City gives the lower limestone a little reflected warmth and lights a sparse, fixed selection of office windows. The cornices follow the ambient City light. [2017 Downtown Counts, p. 10](https://ctycms.com/ga-atlanta/docs/downtown-counts_2017-reduced.pdf#page=10) · [Owner’s October 2018 park photograph](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/611fd546857f6c1cf9a3c938/1642792960214-6LDCCHF85NNYY7EKYGRQ/20181025_AHC_Party_With_The_Past_23.jpg) · [1959 night view, Georgia State Library](https://digitalcollections.library.gsu.edu/digital/collection/lane/id/16091/)
