# 25 Park Place from Woodruff Park

Georgia State’s former Trust Company tower and its three-floor Creative Media Industries Institute pavilion occupy one T-shaped building record. City extruded both to 115 m. At the Edgewood end of the block that obscured the narrow end of the tower rising behind the pavilion. The university identifies the Art Deco building across Equitable Place as the 58 Edgewood annex. It and 55 Park Place have their own footprints. [Deployed City before](production-before.jpg) · [Georgia State’s description of the tower](https://calendar.gsu.edu/25_park_place_buildingsuntrust_tower) · [University account of the pavilion and annex](https://news.gsu.edu/2017/03/03/renovation-projects-transform-campus-near-woodruff-park/)

**Stone, glass and scale.** A photograph from Woodruff Park shows closely spaced pale vertical fins, a horizontal sill at each floor and a band of narrow slots across the crown. The Auburn corner view shows the short elevation, the deeper ground-floor piers and glazing set back from the street. The preservation survey identifies the tower’s white marble exterior. Georgia State counts 26 floors, while the baked record lists 28. The model uses the university count for the lobby and occupied floors, then adds the screened crown. The modeled bay spacing, fin projection, glazing colours and lobby setbacks are photographic estimates. [Woodruff Park elevation](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:25_Park_Place_in_downtown_Atlanta,_view_from_Woodruff_Park.jpg) · [Auburn corner photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:25_Park_Place_Tower,_Atlanta,_GA_(32532495287).jpg) · [Historic preservation survey](https://ctycms.com/ga-atlanta/docs/modern-downtown-atlanta-finalcompressed.pdf)

**Roof measurements.** In the 2018 lidar, 7,601 returns fell inside the merged footprint. The tower measures about 25 × 57 m in plan. Its flat roof is near 431.8 m NAVD88, with a narrow perimeter rim near 432.9 m. That is approximately 114 m above the park-side plaza. The pavilion measures roughly 49 × 27 m. Its terrace and link lie near 331.8 m, its upper roof near 336.7 m and its service equipment near 340 m. The setback glass floor and open terrace are visible from the park. The terrace rails and equipment outlines are simplified. [USGS 2018 Georgia lidar](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json) · [University pavilion photograph](https://cmii.gsu.edu/files/2024/02/CMII-about-banner.jpg) · [Edgewood approach, March 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=5ah2S7DfsqfYy50Wj8D6NA&heading=1&pitch=23)

**After dark.** A 2007 photograph shows warm pools on the projecting tower fins, with a darker crown and scattered lit windows. The university’s dusk pavilion photograph shows warm glazing behind the terrace. The two lighting treatments in City are illustrative. The photographs establish appearance at those exposures and dates.
