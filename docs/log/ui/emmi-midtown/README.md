# Emmi Midtown

Emmi Midtown, at 1405 Spring Street NW (JPX Works, RJTR, completed 2024), is a 31-floor residential tower with 326 units. It replaces the mapped outline `osm:way:269542143` and keeps both of its parts. The L-shaped tower part `osm:way:1456611142` holds Spring Street on the west and 18th Street on the north. The `osm:way:1450431130` part in the southeast corner is the parking podium with the pool deck. The 2019 lidar predates the building, so the height is the survey estimate: 102.3 m for 31 floors. The podium keeps its mapped 36 m.

## References

- [Glass Systems project page (Rick Holliday photograph)](https://glasssystems.biz/projects/emmi-midtown/): the street corner. Blue reflective curtain wall in a dark frame, with charcoal panels staggered across the glass in runs a module wide. The page says the pattern references the letters of the building's name.
- [Urbanize Atlanta photo tour, July 2024](https://atlanta.urbanize.city/post/emmi-midtown-photos-tour-modern-elegant-new-emmi-tower): the full height from a distance (second angle). It shows the paler main wing, a darker second wing and the stacked balcony slot on the narrow end. It also shows the Spring Street canopy entrance and the resident parking portal.
- [RJTR project page](https://rjtrdesign.com/jpx-1405-spring/): the hero rendering (frame grid, dark panel runs, balcony stack) and a dusk photograph of the pool deck on the podium with warm lit apartments above.

The signage on the canopy is left off.

## Estimates

- Floors: a 5.6 m lobby, then 30 floors at 3.17 m to about 100.7 m, with a 1.6 m frame parapet to 102.3 m.
- Modules: 3.0 m on the Spring Street wing and 2.4 m on the 18th Street wing, which also has darker glass. That separates the two elevations, as in the distant photograph. Frame lines run every second floor so the grid stays even at the 300–650 m orbit.
- The pattern of dark panel runs is stylised: two or three floors tall, on alternate modules. It is not a reproduction of the letterforms.
- The balcony slot sits on the south end of the Spring Street wing, with a pale slab line each floor.
- Podium: louvre screen between pale fins at about 3.4 m spacing, a frame band at the deck, and the pool in its southeast quarter. The pool position is estimated from the RJTR dusk photograph.

## Lighting

There is no full-night exterior of the tower. The RJTR dusk photograph shows warm interior light in many apartments and lamps on the pool deck. The City night view uses residential occupancy: about a quarter of the two-module units lit, stable per building, plus a warm lobby along the streets. The garage screen stays dark. Pool-deck lamps are not drawn.
