The two ground maps contain 69 eligible residential ways, 83 mapped sidewalk ways and 33 mapped crossing ways. The mean paver colour survives on the phone map. High adds muted hex joints. Granite edging and liriope bands follow the existing carriageways. At the Prado circle and fork, the grass outlines follow the mapped street loops and inferred 12 m road width. Four illustrative high-tier signs stand on sampled ground, 0.86–1.38 m outside the nearest residential carriageway edge.

Sam’s neighbourhood observations informed the materials and markings. Exact paver spacing, planting, island contours and sign poses are illustrative. The crossing ways locate pedestrian routes. Their painted stripes are clipped to the roads they cross. The work uses the existing board textures and high-tier landscape mesh. A normal web build and coordinator deployment publish the changes.
