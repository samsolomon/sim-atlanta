# Ellis Hotel

The hotel at 176 Peachtree Street has a three-level cast stone base, a red-brown brick shaft with paired sash, a pale terra-cotta crown and a deep dark cornice. The model includes the Peachtree balcony and keeps the south party wall mostly brick, with a narrow middle stack of window pairs.

- The National Register nomination documents the 15-storey building, the 63 × 70 ft plan, the cast stone and brick elevations and the restored balcony. The photographed Peachtree elevation shows five paired sash groups across the shaft. [National Register nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/be9da123-7c33-46ca-8fca-c494383ef6dd) · [Peachtree daylight photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vertical_panorama_of_the_Ellis_Hotel_in_Downtown_Atlanta_taken_from_across_Peachtree_Street.jpg)
- The renovation architect's dusk photos show warm storefront lighting and amber uplights at several brick piers above the base. The model estimates their reach at 6.4 m and uses an illustrative pattern for lit rooms at full night. [Peachtree dusk photo](https://www.ssoe.com/wp-content/uploads/ssoe-sw_ellis-hotel_1300x615.png) · [Peachtree and Ellis dusk corner](https://www.ssoe.com/wp-content/uploads/ssoe-sw_ellis-hotel_465x500-1.png)
- The April 2019 3DEP sample contains 1,108 interior roof returns. The lower main roof is 376.3 m NAVD88, with a raised roof at 379.8 m and small interior plant reaching 386.3 m. The adjacent median ground is 328.08 m, putting the main roof about 48.2 m above grade, versus the generic 60.7 m. The model estimates the widths of the raised rim and plant from point clusters.
- The model uses the staged rectangle, about 21.4 × 19.3 m. Balcony projection, cornice profile and sash dimensions are estimates from the photos. Pale cast-stone belt courses and the two cornice ledges represent the mouldings at City scale.
