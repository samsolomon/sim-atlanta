# College Football Hall of Fame

From Centennial Olympic Park the long panes of translucent glass lead to a copper-colored rotunda. Looking straight at the entrance reveals a recess cut through the curved skin, with a large window, a sloping silver sill and a two-line building name. The concrete support forks above the entrance. The model uses the architect’s front and dusk photographs and the glass supplier’s opposite oblique view. [TVS project photographs](https://www.tvsdesign.com/projects/project-detail/chick-fil-a-college-football-hall-of-fame/) · [Glass supplier’s exterior and glazing study](https://trosifol.kuraray.com/en/projects/football-hall-of-fame/) · [Museum’s account of the building and indoor field](https://www.cfbhall.com/news-and-happenings/news/college-football-hall-of-fame-celebrates-construction-milestone/)

The 21-vertex Overture outline `e7b9bf31-89f1-4e38-b8bd-82cab014f1fd` spans 79.3 × 136.0 m on the City axes. The generic bake gives the entire outline a single 24 m height. Inside it, 6,561 lidar returns place the north service roof near 324.7 m NAVD88, the shallow field roof at roughly 326–330 m and the top of the rotunda near 337 m. The copper profile, inset window, depth of the support and spacing of the translucent panels are photo-scaled. [USGS Georgia 2018 elevation survey](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/GA_Statewide_B2_2018/ept.json)

Chay Reddy’s September 2019 park-side night panorama shows white light along the field hall and above the entrance. The copper skin reads dark behind the lit name. In the architect’s dusk photograph, the transparent ground floor is darker than the opal panes above it. Panel-to-panel variation and intensity are illustrative.
