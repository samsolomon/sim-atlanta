# Square on 5th

`osm:way:536884432`, 848 Spring Street NW. This is 25-storey student housing near Georgia Tech, designed by The Preston Partnership and built by JE Dunn. It topped out in January 2015 and opened that fall. City code: `web/src/city/midtown-skyline-square5th.ts`, registered in the Midtown skyline registry.

## What City draws

The frame runs u east and v south, with the origin at the slab centre (City 219.5, −2681.5).

- **Massing:** the mapped slab, about 60 × 24 m. The south face bows out 3.5 m along the mapped outline.
- **Base:** two brick storeys, 0–8 m, with shopfront and office glazing, and a grey canopy on the Spring Street end. The height is estimated.
- **North face:**
  - 12 m brick end bays with punched windows on a 3 m bay. The bay is estimated.
  - A teal glass centre with a white slab edge at each floor (the stacked balconies).
  - Four light concrete fins, about 9 m apart, running from the base past the roof. Their spacing is estimated from the street photograph.
- **South face:** teal glass with white floor lines along the whole curve, except a brick bay of punched windows at the west end.
- **East and west ends:** brick with punched windows.
- **Roof:** the 88 m lidar roof. The fins carry a thin roof frame to 89.5 m, the lidar maximum, overhanging both broad faces. A brick penthouse stands at the east end.
- **Floors:** 23 residential floors at about 3.4 m.

The pool deck, pergola and balcony rails are too small to read at City distances.

## Night

Student rooms light at 38%, an estimate for 23:30. Base shopfronts and offices light at 30%. Nothing above the residential floors lights. No night photograph was found, so City adds no crown or frame lighting.
