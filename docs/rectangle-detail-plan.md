## Elevation audit

All 16 shared edges measured **0.00 m** of raw-grid and rendered-ground disagreement at 2.5 m spacing on the loaded low-detail board. Every available 5 m ground node was compared with its row's staged 3DEP bare-earth grid in NAVD88. City scene zero is 323.8 m. North of z −1700, low-detail triangulation alone explains differences near ±0.20 m away from the engineered cuts and approaches. A large signed difference at a portal can be intentional. At Leonard Tate, for example, the display removes the overhead freeway return to expose the street below. The default-board owner handles any new seam or plinth issue. [Ground surface and openings](../web/src/terrain/surface.ts) · [Leonard Tate correction](../web/src/city/sweet-auburn-transport.ts)

The [full camera-linked elevation register](rectangle-elevation.csv) records every location flagged by these checks, grouped by row and feature. Terrain entries mark the largest absolute departure in each adjoining group of nodes exceeding 1 m. Canopy entries compare low-tier candidate sites to raw earth before tree rejection. For every **drawn** trunk its root meets the displayed surface within 0.001 m. Roof entries compare the generic horizontal roof plane to ground at exterior and courtyard ring vertices. Bridge entries use the lowest road-specific tread minus displayed ground along each tagged bridge centerline, sampled at ≤5 m. Roof and tread entries require burial deeper than 0.25 m. Crossing entries give upper minus lower **pavement** at interior, non-parallel centerline intersections below 3.5 m. That screening threshold flags possible grade conflicts, including at-grade junctions; it does not measure soffit clearance. Clearances and floor elevations require class-17 deck returns, sections or photographs before changing a structure. The register contains 372 measurements. A candidate can appear under more than one check.
