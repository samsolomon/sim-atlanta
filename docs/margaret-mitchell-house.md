# Margaret Mitchell House

The Crescent Apartments footprint measures 26.0 × 16.2 m. Crescent Avenue is downhill to the west. Its stacked balconies serve different floors from the broad porch facing the Peachtree lawn. Highsmith's lawn-side photograph resolves the projecting sash bay, diagonal half-timbering, hipped roof and chimney behind the cross gable. The History Center's Crescent photograph distinguishes the individual balconies. [Library of Congress, lawn elevation](https://www.loc.gov/item/2011631580/) · [Atlanta History Center, Crescent elevation](https://www.atlantahistorycenter.com/learning-research/projects-initiatives/margaret-mitchell-house-at-atlanta-history-center-midtown/) · [Mapped three-floor building](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/234733558)

The 5 m 3DEP grid gives 293.4–296.4 m NAVD88 around 48 perimeter samples at intervals of at most 2.5 m. The west threshold sets the model datum. The east stair samples its own lawn grade. Roof ridge 14.8 m, chimney cap 15.8 m and lawn porch deck 3.55 m above the west reference are estimates. Window divisions, rail spacing and porch heights come from photographs. Midtown Alliance's 2023 photograph shows luminous garden lamps, holiday lights on tree trunks and a much darker brick wall. AJC reports the seasonal installation was omitted in 2025. Ordinary full-night coverage of both façades remains a gap. City uses restrained garden lights and a few illuminated sash windows. Their colour and occupancy are illustrative.
