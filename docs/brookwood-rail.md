## Exterior and lighting

Amtrak's street-facing photograph shows three arched openings with green joinery, cream surrounds, brick piers, a pale lettered frieze and deep eaves. The October 2022 oblique view shows the short return and the roof silhouette. A March 2025 daylight Street View panorama from the bridge confirms all three facade bays, the hipped roof, the sidewalk barriers and the gateway arches. This was the latest dated daylight facade verified. The March 26, 2026 AP platform view shows bright pale light under a shallow canopy, visible cross-braces and two locomotive headlights. A March 2019 view from the same platform has a distinctly warmer cast. Full-night street-facade lighting is unverified. Canopy extent, details of the lift enclosure, parapet, sash dimensions and fixture spacing are estimated. [March 2025 bridge approach](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=ARLzFEJcxvf6NUAmPW54DQ&heading=254&pitch=2&fov=70) · [Amtrak exterior](https://www.greatamericanstations.com/stations/atlanta-ga-atl/) · [October 2022 return facade](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peachtree_station,_Atlanta,_Georgia.jpg) · [Amtrak access](https://www.amtrak.com/stations/atl) · [Passenger account and stair photos](https://www.hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/Nave-html/S20/trainvis.html) · [March 2026 platform](https://apnews.com/article/e4d8ea591b3b036142c2bf2dee7dff5a) · [March 2019 platform](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amtrak%27s_Crescent_arrives_at_Peachtree_station_in_Atlanta,_Georgia.jpg)

The North Bridge carries two major gateway arches 30 ft (9.14 m) high, and 20 minor arches. The span also has barrier-separated sidewalks and decorative lighting. The model estimates the rib spacing and lean from the March 2025 panorama. The approach bank samples are 274.04 and 272.88 m NAVD88 on the staged grid. ## City integration and inspection

Stage the existing Midtown data with `pnpm data:midtown` and rebuild the web bundle with `pnpm build`. Opening `/?view=city&board=midtown&site=brookwood&q=high&t=2026-11-23T08:50:00-05:00&speed=0` mounts the connected rows and frames Brookwood. Change the hour to `23:10:00` for the northbound call. On an ordinary Midtown visit the south row shows the southwest rail approach. Its clipping plane opens northward as the station row loads. No additional feeds or server requests per train are required.

Both captures use City target (−92, −5054), bearing −65°, tilt 66°, camera distance 165 m, 0.1293 m per CSS pixel at 999 × 496. [Day](log/ui/brookwood/station-day.jpg) · [Night](log/ui/brookwood/station-night.jpg)

The matched track grade uses nine open-track DEM anchors. For the rendered surface we cap high returns within 10 m of each mapped rail and feather the adjustment to 16 m. Lower terrain beneath the railway bridge is retained. The railhead is 0.32 m above the modeled bed and the low boarding surface is 0.53 m above it. The modeled step from railhead to platform is 0.21 m. Platform lip geometry allows 0.15 m of clearance from a 3 m wide modeled car, correcting up to about 0.5 m of disagreement between the independently traced OSM ways. Canopy length (80 m), shelter lighting and light through the waiting-room glazing are illustrative. The two street approaches join the 34 m span to the terrain at the banks. The estimated upper floor is level with the forecourt.

The component replaces the named generic depot and Peachtree bridge after construction succeeds. Cars use the shared `Schedule` output, follow Main 1 in both directions and ease toward the northeast end of the island as a complete consist. A timetable edition determines calls and cancellations. On selection the existing train card retains the GTFS source and tier. On-site labels identify the inferred berth and illustrative consist. [Midtown staging and tile bounds](midtown-board.md)

Architect and product design reviews, 23 November. The imagery and DEM require separate street and track heights. The existing dated schedule, City train selection and shared Midtown mount support the station without a second timetable or a freestanding preview.

## Corridor to the board edges

The survey clip ends at x −575 and +410. The two NS Norcross mains then continue for 583–585 m to the west board edge at x −1000 (z −4190 and −4195). To the northeast they continue for 376–385 m, leaving through the north edge near x 697–703, z −5700. The siding continues another 394 m beside them to x 709. These five mapped OSM runs now join the surveyed lines end to end and are drawn in the same ballast, tie and rail style. They stay in the row data for building and tree clearance and for the road crossing, but the generic drawing is skipped. The Atlanta Terminal Subdivision stub in the northwest corner keeps its generic drawing. [Joins](../web/src/city/brookwood-corridor.ts)

No mapped bridge, tunnel or overpass crosses either continuation, and the 3DEP profile is gentle, so the track runs at grade. The only crossing is an at-grade service road near (−760, −4417). The bed follows 30 open-track anchors. Each one is the median of both mains on the staged 5 m bare-earth grid within 6 m of its x. Going west the anchors climb from 271.7 m at x −600 to 277.2 m at the edge, about 1.3%. Going northeast they fall from 258.45 m at x 430 to 257.25 m at x 580, then rise to 257.9 m at the edge. Both ends meet the survey table within 0.45 m. Within 16 m of a continuation, high ground is trimmed to bed, as inside the survey. Dips within 5 m are also filled, feathered out to 9 m. The fill reaches 20 m inside each end of the survey and nowhere near the freeway rail bridge. Along every 2 m centreline sample, the rendered ground at both detail tiers stays between 0.11 and 0.28 m below bed. Ballast, tie and rail heights between anchors, and the crossing surface, are inferred. [Grade script](../pipeline/prototypes/brookwood-corridor/grade.py) · [Anchors](../web/src/city/fixtures/brookwood-corridor.json)

The extension adds about 10k triangles to the existing Brookwood mesh and no draw calls. Capping takes 13–14 ms per row at mount. Trains do not yet run beyond the survey clip.
