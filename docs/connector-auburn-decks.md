## Two street crossings and an underpass

The lidar classifies a continuous elevated highway across Auburn, Edgewood and John Wesley Dobbs. Several mapped mainline and ramp ways between the bridge tags run on that same elevated structure. A straight chord between bare-earth heights put the southbound freeway within half a metre of the street at Auburn and below the street at Edgewood. ## Reproducing the heights

The offline window contains 965,599 returns acquired on April 22, 2019 UTC, including 108,906 class-17 bridge-deck returns. The bake takes a 3 m radius median at stations up to 10 m apart along each directed road. It requires five returns and rejects a group with a 10th–90th percentile spread above 1.1 m. It interpolates isolated samples across a consistent grade and connects compatible abutment samples to class-2 ground. Leonard Tate uses classified ground returns for the upper surface. Three grade-separated ground ramps below flyovers exclude the overhead returns. On the southbound entrance 38974039 a 301 m sample on the far side anchors the ramp below a 314 m flyover. Source picks are lidar-derived. The abutments, interpolation through occlusions and lateral shape are inferred. [Source way](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/38974039) · [Survey script](../pipeline/prototypes/connector-footprint/deck-survey.py)

The matched profiles contain 446 stations over 62 runs on 60 directed ways, totalling 3.59 km of travel surface including the separate ramps. Install the pipeline Python dependencies plus NumPy, SciPy, pyproj and laspy with LAZ support. Run `python pipeline/prototypes/connector-footprint/deck-survey.py` to reproduce the checked-in values. The upper profiles and corrected ground nodes are bundled with the Sweet Auburn preview. A web rebuild picks them up.
