## Geometry contract

Each directed route owns station, tangent, signed offsets of all edges, vertical datum, grade, crossfall, lane identity and source confidence. Physical crossings have distinct upper and lower carriers. A graph connection needs a shared traversable node. Store measured footprint, abutment skew, traffic direction, inventory span and class-17 deck samples from the site. Use class-2 returns on exposed approach pavement and the rendered lower surface. Interpolate occluded sections between returns on the same carrier.

**Road bridge.** Form a width-varying slab from the measured outline and cross-sections. Place abutment faces at the two skew ends and join approaches to the entire end edge. Carry the vehicle and foot paths on the same pavement. Cut the fascia at confirmed ramp mouths. Give sidewalks their mapped side and width, carry crosswalks through mouths and stop parapets at openings. Seat pier feet in the terrain and bearing tops against the triangulated underside. At 17th the 37 sections rise from roughly 276.3 to 280.5 and descend to 276.25 m NAVD88. The inventory at north Peachtree describes a continuous two-span steel bridge, 113.7 m long and 34 m wide. Parapet and rail heights of 0.73 and 1.17 m and 1.25–2.5 m haunch depth are 17th modeling estimates. A 0.15 m curb and 3.55 m lane pitch are fallback dimensions. Derive shoulder and walk widths from imagery when available. [17th Street](seventeenth-street.md) · [North Peachtree](brookwood-rail.md) · [Sidewalk dimensions](connector-bridge-sidewalks.md)

**Rail bridge.** Keep the verified rail-head grade and gauge. Fit girder depth, webs, diaphragms, seats, bents and end closures around the observed bay. Choose material from underside and side imagery. Fit each bearing face to the actual soffit facets. Preserve the measured lower travel envelope. Track which visible parts are inferred, especially the hidden southbound footing at I-85. [Numeric lessons and source galleries](road-rail-profiles.md)

**Walking and grade.** Every walking strip must identify its supporting street deck, separate documented walking bridge or triangulated ground, including each transition and tile seam. A path through a grass parcel follows the ground. At Capitol Gateway several raised strips visibly cross the grass in the marked build. Record the unsupported length and vertical separation along both edges as a sidewalk defect. For roads choose graded ground, a retained cut/fill, an open bridge or a covered passage from the registered section and lower use. A terrain depression by itself cannot establish an opening.

## Checks proposed for every crossing

Run these on the assembled render triangles on both tiers, after ground carving, with source ID, station, extrema and a camera for the first failure. Numerical tolerances below are proposed rendering budgets, distinct from measured physical clearance.

| Check | Failure condition |
|---|---|
| Deck and approach | Find open seams across the entire width, including skew corners. Require common seam vertices within 1 mm and no unsupported interval. Flag vertical steps above 2 mm at a modeled flush joint. Sample grades on both sides and compare to the source envelope. Ellis previously dropped 12 cm and the correction reduced the first metre to 0.84% grade. |
| Sidewalk | Trace both walk edges and the accessible route from ground onto deck and off again. Fail on an open end, unsupported span, missing landing or crossing of the wrong carriageway. Compare signed tread-to-support distance to the intended curb or surface offset. |
| Clearance and supports | Intersect the entire underside with all lower travel surfaces, including elevated service roads and rail. Fail inverted level order, intersection, or a clearance below the documented site target. Report unresolved physical clearance separately. Require complete toe seating and bearing contact to 1 mm numerical tolerance. Flag any positive pier area in a lane or shoulder with signed area and a photo disposition. |
| Tapers and moving stock | Count and trace lane intervals across every opening, split and merge. Fail duplicated solids/dashes, markings outside the tread, reversed lane order or a vehicle traversing the gore. Check individual tyre contacts on the drawn surface and chassis roll. Record existing wheel residuals for inherited rolling stock. |
| Internal seams and clips | Load adjacent tiles in both orders. Verify the same route, train, draw ownership and clock from head entry through tail exit in both directions. Fail disappearing cars, hanging asphalt edges, interior abutments or striping breaks at source changes. Check the full oblique exterior cut. |
| Regression and cost | Keep normal board, low side and reverse cameras and run before/after with identical matrices and clock. Archive worst-contact sections and moving replays. Compare Low performance, draw calls and bytes on the same mounted data. |
