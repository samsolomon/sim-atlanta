# Radio sleeves

[Contact sheet](contact-sheet.png) shows each sleeve at 64 px and 256 px. Each artist has a 1024 px PNG master and two WebP exports.

[Round 3 comparison](contact-sheet-round3.png) shows both Lanternfish editions and the four Feltwork choices at 256 and 64 px. Sam selected option A, the overhead photograph of a mustard-yellow umbrella, as the final Feltwork sleeve.

[Round 2 comparison](contact-sheet-v1-vs-v2.png) pairs each archived master with its replacement at both sizes.

The built-in image generator received standalone prompts for Velour Avenue and the Round 2 and Round 3 artwork. For the other sleeves and the archived versions, the prompt begins with the shared text below, followed by a space and the artist-specific paragraph.

> Original square 1024x1024 album sleeve artwork, full bleed, no mockup or borders. One strong silhouette or color idea legible at 64 pixels. No lettering, logos, brands, recognizable people, references to existing covers, cities, trains or maps.

## Feltwork

Glasshouse 94.1 · A mustard-yellow umbrella crosses dark, wet pavement.

> Square full-bleed 35 mm street photograph as a quiet instrumental record sleeve. Camera pointing vertically down: one anonymous person almost entirely hidden by a single mustard-yellow umbrella on rain-dark asphalt, with ample empty pavement. Umbrella canopy spans about one quarter of the frame, offset to the right; at left a few worn painted crossing stripes enter the crop at a diagonal. Distorted puddle reflections, soft overcast daylight, muted slate charcoal tones punctuated by mustard. Calm observational photography, delicate grain. Make the umbrella and broad street markings read cleanly at 64 px. No faces, other people, text, logos or recognizable locations.

### B · Golden light catches the wool on an upright piano’s hammers.

> Square full-bleed intimate photograph taken inside an upright piano, looking diagonally down the hammer rail. In extreme close-up a receding row of substantial rounded ivory wool felt hammer heads, a thin stripe of wine-red felt and warm wooden shanks; copper-wound strings farther back. Shallow depth of field, central few hammers in focus, falloff into soft dark amber, golden side light catching the felt nap. Make the repeating pale hammer crowns a strong rhythmic band cutting across deep shadows, identifiable even at 64 px. Cosy tactile analog film photograph of a real working mechanism. No keyboard, people, text, labels or brand marks. Original album sleeve.

## Lanternfish

Glasshouse 94.1 · A warm horizon light casts a long golden reflection across the dark water. Edited from v2.

> Edit this square Lanternfish sleeve. Preserve the large empty sky, still open sea, small restrained upper-left 'Lanternfish' type exactly as spelled, and solitary far-right horizon light. Lift the sky to a deep dusk gradient from indigo overhead to smoky mauve and a faint warm peach band along the horizon. Enlarge and warm the solitary light so its core and small golden halo together read at 64 px. Give it a long luminous golden reflection reaching almost the entire way down the water, a softly broken tapering ribbon broad enough to survive thumbnailing. Keep the water calm, the photographic grain subtle, the mood spare. No other objects or text. Full bleed original ambient album sleeve, square.

## Café Interlock

Sol Café 101.7 · A cropped guitar performance meets a block of black type in two-color ink.

> Original 1960s bossa nova jazz record sleeve designed in two printing inks, lemon yellow and rich black. A bold offset-printed duotone performance photograph taken from the side: tight diagonal crop of anonymous hands playing a classical nylon-string guitar, one plucking by the sound hole and one fretting, the instrument and arms continue beyond the edges; no faces. Asymmetric editorial layout interlocking photo crop and bold flat lemon-yellow area. A very strong left-aligned block of tightly set heavy black grotesque sans capitals reads exactly 'CAFÉ INTERLOCK' across two lines, acute accent on É, plus smaller mixed-case 'Lemon Ice'. Type is an equal partner with the musician, with ample contrast. Authentic ink bite. Square flat full bleed, no extra typography, logos or imitation of any specific album.
