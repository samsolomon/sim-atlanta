## Piedmont Atlanta Hospital

- The southeast corner photograph shows a sharp glass prow, a terracotta return and a continuous warm horizontal break. The plaza photograph faces north along the glass wall and shows the shallow canopy at the main doors, the timber soffit and the double-height frontage. [HKS corner and plaza](https://www.hksinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/caseStudy-Piedmont2-b1.jpg) · [HKS plaza looking north](https://www.hksinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/caseStudy-Piedmont2-b4.jpg) · [HGOR streetscape](https://www.hgor.com/project/piedmont-atlanta-hospital/)
- The hospital site plan places the main entrance and valet on Peachtree near the north end of the plaza. It also separates the South Deck, the rear North Deck, employee parking and 275. The model reuses the surrounding core, office and garage masses, including the existing separate Shepherd Center and east-side Building 2001 context. [Piedmont campus map](https://www.piedmont.org/media/file/2022-08-PAH-Campus-Map.pdf) · [Accessible campus plan from Piedmont’s interactive map](https://interactive.medmaps.com/pdf/public_pdfs/25)
- The contractor's southeast dusk photograph shows a continuous warm light at the break in the glass skin, a bright lobby and irregular illuminated panes concentrated below the break. The model uses the same broad lighting pattern. Individual rooms, colour temperature and brightness are estimates. The photo shows dusk, so the full-night model is a continuation of the dusk treatment. [Brasfield & Gorrie dusk exterior](https://www.brasfieldgorrie.com/wp-content/uploads/external/d26a77a3e70ed10cb477193f9855080e.jpg)
- HKS, Piedmont and structural engineer Uzun+Case report 16 stories. The mapped Marcus Tower and podium cover approximately 4,159 m² and 5,796 m² respectively. The private context estimates their heights at 57.6 m and 14.4 m. The model uses these values above the entrance grade. The connected six-floor core, ten-floor Building 275 and eight-floor Shepherd Center use the board's source tags. No independent roof elevations were found. The 35.1 m spandrel break, 8.1 m glazed base and roughly 20 × 7 m canopy are visual estimates. ### Corner, North Parking and older wings

- **Peachtree and Collier.** The lower walk now follows the grade around a curved planting bed and joins the inside edge of the mapped sidewalk. The low curb follows the bed perimeter. The pavement stops short of the crossing spur, and the bed stays roughly 9.5 m inside the sidewalk centerline at its closest point. The bed outline and curb reveal are photo-derived estimates. The planting abstracts the low grasses and shrubs visible in an October 2025 Street View frame and the architect’s corner photograph. [HKS corner photograph](https://www.hksinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/caseStudy-Piedmont2-b1.jpg) · [HGOR landscape plan and photographs](https://www.hgor.com/project/piedmont-atlanta-hospital/)
- **North Parking.** The parking contractor’s photos identify the open concrete bands, the roof deck, and the enclosed pedestrian link beside the uphill entrance. The deck now uses the existing open parking geometry on its mapped outline. Seven levels including the roof, 22.1 m above the low foundation, and the link floor 5.8 m above the drive are modeling estimates. The 10.6 m link axis follows the mapped bridge between the North Deck and the hospital core. The glazing is modeled from the daylight photo. Full-night lighting on the link is unverified. [Parking contractor photos, pp. 2–4](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/627ff551173a307442a0426d/t/666b53433d1c817564cf1fc1/1718309705513/Piedmont%2BHospital%2B-%2BAtlanta%2C%2BGA%2B-%2B2000.docx.pdf) · [Piedmont campus map](https://interactive.medmaps.com/pdf/public_pdfs/25)
- **Older wings.** Building 105 was an 8 m fallback despite the five-level silhouette and 5xxx suites. It now uses five nominal floors, 18 m above its drawn foundation, with the existing generic façade. The main older complex remains the six-level mapped mass. Building 275 and Shepherd remain distinct on the board. The South Deck has a mapped mass. Piedmont’s floor plan shows a South Deck bridge, but the available exterior views and mapped linework do not pin its span or elevation well enough for an exterior model. [Piedmont clinic directory](https://services.piedmont.org/georgia/east-point) · [Hospital floor map](https://piedmontcareers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2022-10-PAH-Department-Map.pdf)

### Peachtree frontage and garden

The short Collier wall at the southeast tip now has an estimated 9.4 m glazed return and a 12.3 m terracotta bay, measured along the mapped face. The glass wraps onto Peachtree and rises about 5.2 m higher than the clay bay. The ground-floor glass sets back 1.05 m along the court and up to 2.8 m at the corner below the timber soffit. This split comes from the corner shown in the July 2022 Street View at 3 Collier and an October 2025 Peachtree view. The angle changes the apparent proportions. The two photos and HKS’s oblique exterior show where the two materials meet and how they turn at the prow. The dimensions are visual estimates. [HKS corner view](https://www.hksinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/caseStudy-Piedmont2-b1.jpg) · [Contractor dusk from the intersection](https://www.brasfieldgorrie.com/wp-content/uploads/external/d26a77a3e70ed10cb477193f9855080e.jpg)

The new court follows the long concave lobby and stops short of the northern entrance landing. The lower paved walk and upper sidewalk strip use the drawn 3DEP grade. An inset stair joins those walks alongside the planted slope, with step-free paths at its ends. The garden models nine small elms as an illustrative sample of the pollarded grove in HGOR’s photos. Their individual positions, dimensions, bed mixes, paving and stair elevations are estimated. The selected November clock gives the elms late-autumn colour. [HGOR plaza and design notes](https://www.hgor.com/project/piedmont-atlanta-hospital/) · [HGOR planting and walk](https://www.hgor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/HGORPiedHospital006-e1774463600306.jpg) · [HGOR stair and recessed lobby](https://www.hgor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/HGORPiedHospital079-e1774463820885.jpg)
