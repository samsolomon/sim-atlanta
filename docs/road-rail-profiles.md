# Road and rail profiles

These are authoring rules for the City miniature. Dimensions below describe individual modeled sites. Store the source, datum, uncertainty and date per attribute. Compare the final Low and High triangles, including painted ground and moving stock.

| Rule | Brookwood or reference evidence |
|---|---|
| Give the running surface, edge, stripes, gore and vehicle paths one set of directed cross-sections. Carry a through lane continuously through a split. Map a shared option lane to both recipients when the turn evidence supports it. | At Exit 86 six northbound positions feed five through lanes and one exit. The gore opens 100–145 m downstream of the source node. On the opposing five-plus-one entrance the separator changes to dashed 25–10 m upstream. Those intervals come from the 2024 image. A constant five-lane section is a control for stripe continuity. |
| Fit the tangents and width change at both ends. Open or end each stripe at its own physical station. | At the marked southbound entrance the heading change over one metre fell from 16.65° to 0.29° at the outside edge and from 16.43° to 0.40° at the gore. All 244 stock centres matched the expected route. [Entrance comparison](http://127.0.0.1:5218/study/brookwood-entrance-alignment/) |
| Where a carriageway has a coherent grade and its outside edge falls off the terrain grid, fit a graded surface across the whole pavement. Extend fill to the surrounding ground. Add a retaining face where the source shows one. | At station 270 of I-85 southbound way 38983384 the modeled High crossfall went from −40.65% to +3.98% across 17.75 m. Ground checked the trail opening at 2,395 columns per tier. A wall toe or fill slope beyond the measured pavement is an estimate. |
| Use a deck over a documented open passage and a lined bore or cover where imagery and crossing order support it. Measure the lower route on its own tread and preserve the portals. | The trail runs over GA-13 and through a separate bore beneath I-85. Its approximately 7 m modeled headroom is a scene dimension. At the MARTA flyover the actual rendered ramp tread gives 4.647 m to the soffit. [Trail](http://127.0.0.1:5218/study/northeast-trail/) · [MARTA walls](http://127.0.0.1:5218/study/marta-i85-roadside-protection/) |
| Build the road deck as one skew slab. Join incoming pavement across the whole mouth. Continue sidewalks along the street, across a supported deck or on drawn ground, including the ends. | The two 17th Street openings measure 18.37 and 18.77 m. Each ramp shares its two corner positions with the slab. At downtown Peachtree the terrain at one deck corner lies 10.5 m below the structure. A separate approach carries the walk and carriageway inland. [17th Street](seventeenth-street.md) · [Junctions](connector-junctions.md) · [Walk edges](connector-bridge-sidewalks.md) |
| Run the rail girders under exposed formation through to full-width contact with the embankments. Preserve the open bay, bearing contacts and wheel path. | At Mayson the four 3.2 m end faces seat at least 0.732 m into the modeled earth. All 48 seam vertices coincide and 4,080 tread centres reproduce control. Up to 0.794 m of outer ballast daylight beyond the caps remains documented. [Mayson sections](http://127.0.0.1:5218/study/mayson-approaches/) |
| Choose the material for the physical structure. Include the visible underside. | The NS bridge shared by freight and Amtrak shows iron-brown flanged webs and diaphragms on concrete bents. Use restrained oxide on exposed steel. The North Line flyover shows pale concrete and round shafts. Brookwood's modeled road opening is at least 7.988 m. Exact bent stations and hidden foundations are inferred. [NS bridge and source photo](http://127.0.0.1:5218/study/brookwood-rail-over-i75/) · [North Line source and shoulder exception](http://127.0.0.1:5218/study/marta-i85-shoulders/) |
| Match physical connectivity across source splits and loaded tile boundaries. Apply clipping at the actual outside perimeter to each car or lane footprint. | NS Main 2 continues 1,069.599 m on the same way past the old internal clip. The old plane cut approximately half the twelfth unit. Verify head and tail in both directions at the internal seam and the exterior edge. The I-85 east edge cuts a 21.35 m pavement obliquely, producing 23.10 and 23.19 m exposed cross-sections. |
