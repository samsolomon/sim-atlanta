# Georgia Tech at North Avenue

The two stadium ways trace the west grandstand and the connected north, east and south stands. The ground layer separately carries Hyundai Field (Historic Grant Field), a roughly 50.5 × 111.8 m quadrilateral. The modeled playing surface is 49 × 109.8 m with goalposts, end zones and hash marks. The modern aerial shows two long sideline banks, a much deeper north end and a low south end at Wardlaw. The east-to-south night view resolves the floodlight banks, fascia, field and south scoreboard. City represents a quiet venue with emissive mast heads and press-box windows. Game-specific scoreboard content and field illumination were not observed for the pinned simulation clock. The 3DEP field-centre reference is 279.3 m NAVD88. At intervals of 2.5 m or less, 156 west perimeter samples span 279.4–297.0 m, and 351 connected-stand samples span 279.1–287.5 m. The terrain audit found the old roofs buried 5.65 m and 0.26 m respectively. The new seating rises from field level toward the higher outside edge, with a retaining face down to the lowest sampled perimeter. Estimated rear heights above field grade are 34 m west, 33 m north, 20 m east and 8.6 m south. Aisle intervals, seating steps, mast heights and the press box are inferred. The modeled turf uses a level surface above the rendered terrain across the pitch. Tech Tower occupies the Evans Administration Building, a separate 40-vertex footprint west of the stadium. The south approach shows the cross-gabled brick building and pointed tower, while the close photograph resolves the triple sash, projecting stone courses, brick gablets and light letters under the steep roof. Georgia Tech's evening photograph shows bright letters along two faces and warm light near the slate. The model lights the four signs and selected windows. The number of lit rooms and the light intensity are illustrative. The mapped perimeter spans 299.5–302.0 m NAVD88 across 85 samples, and the model reference reads 300.8 m. The eaves at 12.6 m, 8.2 m tower width and 39.4 m finial height are estimates. Letter height is approximately 1.5 m.
