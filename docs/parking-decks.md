# Looking across the Connector at Grady’s parking decks

In the saved Grady camera, the broad pale roof immediately to the hospital’s left is the **Grady Butler Parking Deck**. ## Reading the photographs

At Butler, the older exterior photograph shows warm concrete bands extending past the dark parking openings. The Jesse Hill Market occupies part of the ground floor. The aerial shows a broad, pale parking surface with long rows of stalls. At Hackney, the state’s street photograph shows lighter concrete piers and glazed corner bays. The aerial resolves two long ramp openings in its roof. The reverse view from Corey Tower places the garages on the far side of the rail viaduct with Grady behind them.
