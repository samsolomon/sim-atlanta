# From the I-20 approaches to Sweet Auburn

The Connector carries **eight northbound striped positions at the east edge of the downtown square**. The eighth arrives from the entrance alongside the Georgia State railway bridges. Southbound at the same edge, a seven-position mainline and a separate one-lane entrance run alongside one another. They share one pavement for about 22 m before the right lane leaves for MLK Jr. Drive. The two directions reach eight at different places. ## Reading the cross-sections

The [section register](../pipeline/prototypes/connector-footprint/south.json) contains 66 transects and 221 pairs of stripe edges, ordered northward. Five transects cover only the ramps flanking Georgia State. The [original 153 m A/B survey](connector-road-footprint.md) supplies the mainline between S1260 and S1460. Each group records the directed OSM way ID, striped lane count, and median-side and outside edges in City metres. S1100, for example, intersects four separate groups across the same transect.

The geometry was fitted against Fulton County's 2024 Pictometry layer. The county lists the collection year. The exact flight date is unresolved. The aerial was sampled on a 0.2 m output grid in 240 m windows, then rotated into transects normal to the local northbound OSM alignment. The street-level checks below establish direction, lane use and physical separation. At exposed edge lines, allow roughly 1–2 m horizontally. Shadows, painted fork tapers and overpasses require fitting a normal lane pitch to adjacent stripes, with roughly a lane of uncertainty where an entire edge is obscured. Decimal coordinates preserve registration for the renderer. They exceed the imagery's measurement precision. Reproduce a transect by following the northbound OSM ways from the first node of way 119686422, City (−8.95, 1560.21). S1100 is 1,100 m along this line, S2205 is 2,205 m. For a georeferenced visual check, invert City metres using the Five Points origin at 33.7539° N, 84.3916° W and the 6,371,008.8 m sphere. The coordinate pairs in the register directly locate the two stripe edges. Option lanes may serve two destinations until the split. The directional through-lane field is empty in these records. A directional breakdown into through and auxiliary positions therefore uses arrows, markings and OSM turn tags. ## Two different southbound ramps at Edgewood

On the **elevated carriageway**, way 1414809190 brings the left HOV position into the five general-purpose lanes. Its OSM geometry ends at City (1194.16, −80.09), an internal node of the five-lane through way. Between that node and (1177.09, 1.43), six contiguous striped positions carry southbound traffic. A seventh travels along the *outside* entrance, ways 38945230 and 1450888994. Looking south from the deck in July 2026, two white lines separate that entrance on the driver's right. At S2130, the six-position strip is about 21 m wide. A roughly 2 m painted wedge precedes the separate 3.7 m entrance. S2095 and S2110 track the stripe as the wedge closes.
