# The Varsity

The archived outline (OSM way 38977297) has a 28.7 m frontage on North Avenue and runs 50 m back toward the 1962 parking deck, with a narrower dining wing reaching 74 m. Jules Gray's 1940 Streamlined Moderne front survives under the 1994 stainless refit. The front has a cream tile wall over a maroon base, a porthole west of the glass-block entry, a red marquee, and a tapered blade sign rising from it. Gray's 1960s accordion panels, alternating tones with maroon triangles top and bottom, clad the side walls. A long curb-service canopy stands west of the building across the service lane. The 5 m 3DEP grid reads 300.0–300.6 m NAVD88 along the North Avenue front and about 298 m at the back, a 2 m fall. The model datum is the sidewalk at the middle of the front. The side panels, canopy columns and V pole each sample their own ground. The canopy deck follows the fall between its two ends. Estimated from photographs, using a 1.75 m pedestrian for scale: tile wall 5.4 m, wavy parapet crest 6.75–7.35 m, marquee 3.6–4.2 m projecting 2.9 m, and blade crown 15.2 m with an angled cap to 16.3 m. Esri World Imagery (undated) puts the canopy at about 7.5 × 44 m, x 181–188.5 and z −1950…−1994 in Five Points metres, with columns spaced at an estimated 5.4 m. One press source gives the V sign's height as 45 ft (13.7 m). Its location at the lot's North Avenue corner and its facing are estimates: it faces the Connector, turned 25° toward North Avenue. Sources disagree on when it went up. The rooftop flags and their colours are illustrative.

Lettering on the blade and the V is abstract: seven red blocks stand in for the letters, so City draws no logotype. In the 2007 night photograph the blade's letters and frame are red neon with chaser bulbs, the marquee and canopy carry red neon bands, and the storefront glows warm. City reproduces that. Curb service ended in 2020, so the lit canopy shows the 2007 photograph's era. Current full-night coverage remains a gap. Night colours and intensities are illustrative.
