# Crescent equipment at Brookwood

Atlanta sees both the Siemens ALC-42 Charger and the GE P42DC Genesis. At Temple, Georgia, on May 17, 2026, P42 #78 led Charger #318. On August 1 Charger #371 led P42 #132, with the Nashville diner and Elkhart River sleeper farther back. The May 3 southbound even carried an extra Charger on the rear. The timetable gives us the Atlanta call. It does not identify today's engines, car assignments or orientation. [May 17 night approach and pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5juGsUvTL0) · [August 1 night approach, sides and tail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=heLU9MzEm9A) · [May 3 daylight run, three camera positions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ACXDpjVIp8) · [Amtrak timetable](https://content.amtrak.com/content/timetable/Crescent.pdf)

The illustrative train follows the order logged for northbound #20 departing New Orleans on May 24, 2025. Two locomotives lead three coaches, an Amfleet café, a Viewliner diner, a sleeper and a baggage-dorm. That gives nine vehicles over **224.59 m** in the model. A full baggage car is a separate lab study. The mixed locomotive liveries and the choice of a second-generation sleeper draw on other sightings. Amtrak records the return of a dedicated Crescent dining car in June 2024, alongside the café. [May 24 consist and broadside photos](https://neworleansrailroads.com/blog/2025/06/17/crescent-20-phase-vii-expansion/) · [Amtrak FY2026 report, food service](https://www.amtrak.com/content/dam/projects/dotcom/english/public/documents/corporate/reports/Amtrak-General-Legislative-Annual-Report-FY2026-Grant-Request.pdf)

## Scale and silhouettes

Dimensions below are model metres. Length includes couplers, width is across the body and height is from railhead to the main roof. Truck spacing is pivot to pivot. Door centres are approximate longitudinal offsets from vehicle centre, positive toward the front of the model. End diaphragms and passage doors are additional to the side openings.

Amtrak's car data table gives the nominal 85-foot passenger-car length. The Amfleet drawing gives 85 ft 4 in over couplers, 10 ft 6 in wide and 59 ft 6 in between trucks. The Charger envelope is rounded from the Charger platform specification. FRA records Amtrak's statement that the two Charger variants share the body envelope. The remaining dimensions, locomotive pivots, window counts, door offsets and roof fittings are scale estimates. ## Day and night reference plates

- The March 2019 platform approach and March 26, 2026 Atlanta boarding photograph show the high, closely spaced white Genesis headlamps under different canopy lighting. The Temple runbys show the working lead lights away from the platform, uneven bands of passenger-window light, black cargo bays and red markers receding together. Red marker spacing, lamp intensities, ditch-light state, individual blinds and which rooms are lit are illustrative. The available views do not resolve every fixture on both sides of each car.
