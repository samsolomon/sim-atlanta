# Crescent equipment at Brookwood

Atlanta sees both the Siemens ALC-42 Charger and the GE P42DC Genesis. At Temple, Georgia, on May 17, 2026, P42 #78 led Charger #318. On August 1 Charger #371 led P42 #132, with the Nashville diner and Elkhart River sleeper farther back. The May 3 southbound even carried an extra Charger on the rear. The timetable gives us the Atlanta call. It does not identify today's engines, car assignments or orientation. [May 17 night approach and pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5juGsUvTL0) · [August 1 night approach, sides and tail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=heLU9MzEm9A) · [May 3 daylight run, three camera positions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ACXDpjVIp8) · [Amtrak timetable](https://content.amtrak.com/content/timetable/Crescent.pdf)

The illustrative train follows the order logged for northbound #20 departing New Orleans on May 24, 2025. Two locomotives lead three coaches, an Amfleet café, a Viewliner diner, a sleeper and a baggage-dorm. That gives nine vehicles over **224.59 m** in the model. A full baggage car is a separate lab study. The mixed locomotive liveries and the choice of a second-generation sleeper draw on other sightings. Amtrak records the return of a dedicated Crescent dining car in June 2024, alongside the café. ## Charger liveries

The Charger is the Crescent's usual lead. The model wears **Phase VII**, the standard ALC-42 scheme from unit 309 onward. That covers roughly 116 of the 125 units numbered 300–424, about 93%. Phase VII means a black windscreen band and cab roof, red cheeks and cab cap edged by a white arc, and a midnight-blue body. A white swoosh climbs from the sill about 8.6 m behind the nose to the roof just ahead of the grilles, with lighter Amtrak blue behind it. A red sill band runs under a fine white line. Dated Crescent sightings: #359 leading northbound train 20 at Gilbert, Virginia, 1 May 2025; #315 at New Orleans, December 2024; #352 at Armour Yard, Atlanta, 13 January 2025. [Gilbert](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amtrak_Crescent_at_Gilbert,_Virginia,_May_2025.jpg) · [New Orleans](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amtrak_315_at_New_Orleans,_December_2024.jpg) · [Armour Yard](https://www.railpictures.net/photo/875549) · [#363 broadside, 19 Sep 2025](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amtrak_363.jpg) · [#379 head-on, 11 May 2026](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:11.05.26_Roanoke,_VA_379.jpg)

Other schemes still appear:
- **Phase VI**, units 300 and 302–308. These are medium blue, with a silver tail carrying a red chevron and a thin red crescent over the cab. They still lead the Crescent: #305 on train 19 in Atlanta, 3 May 2025, and #304 on a Crescent trip, 26 October 2023. The lab keeps this scheme as `/models.html?v=chargerPhase6`. Sam's reference frame of "#1405" comes from a USA Today video credited to Amtrak, so it is probably a promotional render. No Amtrak locomotive carries that number. The frame matches the Phase VII layout in the dated photographs, which the model follows.

**Front end (from #359 at full resolution, #379 and #317 at dusk):** two windscreen panes with a centre post. Each has one pantograph wiper parked near vertical, both leaning the same way, and a number board in the top outboard corner. Two round LED headlights sit under the centre post on a black shelf. A slanted lamp "eye" on each side holds the outboard ditch light and an inboard red marker. The coupler beam carries a centre plate with a red band, coloured fittings and two red lamps per side over a grey snowplough. Only a leading unit lights the headlights, ditch lights and number boards, and only a trailing one lights the two red rear markers. The boards are blank because the model carries no operator numbers. **Estimated:** livery edge curves and the swoosh angle, fitted against matched lab cameras; the roof hatch; and roof equipment heights. The horn location was not resolved in any photo and is not modelled.

## Car liveries and the roof step

**Heights.** Amfleet II stands 12 ft 8 in (3.86 m) over the roof and Viewliner I and II stand 14 ft (4.27 m), so a Crescent shows a real 0.41 m roof step wherever coaches meet Viewliners. The model keeps it. Both families sit on a floor about 51 in (1.30 m) above the rail, and their lower window rows share 2.48–3.10 m. That is why the stripes on the real train run level across the step, as do the red sill lines at the foot of the fluting, about 1.45 m. The June 2026 Elizabeth photograph shows both effects at the coupling. These heights are secondary-source figures (Wikipedia's Amfleet and Viewliner articles). No CAF or Budd diagram was found. Window and stripe heights are estimates scaled from the photographs. [Amfleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amfleet) · [Viewliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viewliner) · [Train 20 at Elizabeth, 3 Jun 2026](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amtrak_Crescent_train_20_passing_Elizabeth_station_June_2026.jpg)

**Which stripes.** Amtrak began repainting passenger cars into Phase VII in December 2024, as they come through shops.
- **Amfleet II coaches:** two of the three on northbound #20 on 24 May 2025 were already Phase VII, and the observer reports Phase VII coaches on the Crescent "almost daily". The illustrative formation runs two Phase VII coaches and one Phase VI.
- **Phase VI:** the blue band holds the windows and runs about 0.4 m below them. Thin red/white/red pinstripes sit above it, and a red line runs low on the side.
- **Phase VII:** a class-colour panel by the door (green for coach), then a midnight band behind a white diagonal, then Amtrak blue to the far end with one more diagonal. The red sill line carries a fine white line above it.
- **The café:** no Phase VII café has been photographed yet, so it stays Phase VI.
- **Viewliner II diners, sleepers and baggage-dorms** wear **Phase IIIB**: three equal red, white and blue bands spanning the lower window row, visible between the windows and full width on blank panels. The upper windows carry no stripe.
- **Viewliner I sleepers** in Phase VI or Phase VII also run on the Crescent but are not modelled.
- **Viewliner II full baggage car** (lab only): no dated photo of one on the Crescent was found, so it keeps its earlier thin red and blue stripes, unverified.

**AIRO** is a corridor trainset. Its assigned routes are Northeast Regional, Keystone, Carolinian, Palmetto, Piedmont, Cascades and others; none is the Crescent. Airo graphics are not used on the Crescent. The lab carries one Airo coach, `/models.html?v=airoCoach`, so Sam's references are represented and ready for corridor service:
- **Envelope (Siemens Airo data sheet):** 85 ft (25.91 m) long, 10 ft 6 in wide, 4.436 m overall with HVAC housings over a 4.36 m roof crown, floor 51 in, truck centres 59 ft 6 in.
- **East-coast scheme:** taken from Amtrak's promotional renders, the USA Today frames Sam sent. They are reference only and not committed.
  - Midnight-blue door zones at both ends.
  - Two white diagonals that lean the same way.
  - Lighter blue panels above and below a continuous near-black window strip.
  - White over red at floor level, and a silver roof.
  - Green class bars beside each plug door.
- **Estimated:** diagonal positions and lean, door spacing and the window pitch. No free photograph of an east-coast Airo car was found.
- **Proportions:** checked against the first Cascades delivery at Seattle, 16 May 2026.
