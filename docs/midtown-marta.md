# Midtown MARTA stations

## North Avenue

The low entrance at West Peachtree and North Avenue has a concrete fascia spanning an open approach. The street pylon and recessed ceiling lights are visible from the south. The view back from the other end shows two rows of pyramidal skylights and a colored fascia facing the plaza. The plaza has square planters and a stage built from a shipping container. At the next block, three mapped doorways lead into the existing Tower Square building.
