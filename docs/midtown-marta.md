# Midtown MARTA stations

## North Avenue

The low entrance at West Peachtree and North Avenue has a concrete fascia spanning an open approach. The street pylon and recessed ceiling lights are visible from the south. The view back from the other end shows two rows of pyramidal skylights and a colored fascia facing the plaza. The plaza has square planters and a stage built from a shipping container. At the next block, three mapped doorways lead into the existing Tower Square building. ## Midtown station

The Peachtree Place entrance has a deep concrete waffle canopy. The ends of its transverse beams remain visible from the street. Glazed doors and a broad opening alternate with concrete piers. The renovated plaza photograph shows painted plant forms on the piers, benches and low green seating mounds. Across 10th Street two smaller head houses cover the separate entrances. The pavilion outline (OSM 230593407) is archived as a station and missing from the generic row buildings. The model uses that 66 × 43 m outline together with mapped ways 235006952 and 235006968 and the two pedestrian plazas. [Peachtree Place approach](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Midtown_MARTA_Station_-_Peachtree_Place_entrance.jpg) · [Street-side angle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MARTA_Midtown_station.jpg) · [Midtown Alliance's completed plaza](https://www.midtownatl.com/midtown-alliance/blueprint-midtown/midtown-station) · [Night photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/cizauskas/49690144608/)

The night photograph shows light at the street stairs. Recessed lights appear in the daytime canopy soffit. The photograph is monochrome, so color and intensity are illustrative. Local 3DEP grid nodes give 292.1 m NAVD88 at (480, −3025) and 292.8 m at both (465, −3085) and (500, −3085). The canopy soffit sits an estimated 4.35 m above the highest footprint vertex. Rib spacing, art colors, stair enclosure height and lighting are inferred from appearance.
