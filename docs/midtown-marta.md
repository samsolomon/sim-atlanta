## North Line guideway and spans

From the portal to the board's north edge, City now draws the North Line from its own module, with one designed profile shared by both tracks. The generic rail strips skip the North Line runs, which stay in the row data for crossings and placement. On bare earth the ballast tread rides 0.13 m above the highest rendered ground between the outer rails. Where both shoulders stand higher, as in the walled cut at the portal, it takes only the ground under the rails. Wherever a mapped road crosses under a span, rail level stays at least 7.1 m above that road's ground. That leaves 4.9 m beneath the 2.2 m deck and girder. A span over Clear Creek, which has no road beneath, simply joins its banks. A grade-limited envelope (3.5% at most) with a 20 m vertical curve joins the pieces, so each span meets its banks without a step. The first 6 m inside the portal hood take their height from the cut beyond, because there the grid blends in the hillside over the tunnel. Trains and the portal read their height from the same profile.

### Tunnel mouth

The open concrete hood read as a canopy: from above, the hillside showed straight through it. The portal is now a cut-and-cover tunnel mouth. A concrete headwall is set into the slope, with one opening, 5.2 m high, spanning both tracks. The headwall's height comes from the rendered ground 4–10 m behind the mouth plus 0.6 m, at most 11.2 m above the rail. Coping runs along its top. Wing walls splay along the cut for 8 m and step down to the ground. Earth closes over the box, at least 0.4 m over the tube's roof along its full 60 m. It is drawn only where it stands clear of the terrain, and its colour was matched to the rendered ground by pixel sampling. Inside, side walls, a centre wall, the roof and the floor fade from concrete to near-black over 18 m. A dark end cap closes the tube 60 m in. Cars are now clipped 5 m inside the mouth (`NORTH_LINE_VANISH_DEPTH`), so trains enter the dark before they vanish. A test checks that this clip depth is applied. Any Follow state for the North Line's mapped tunnel should begin at this depth. Two small lamps at the crown, one over each track, and two pairs of interior lamps 6 m and 16 m in glow faintly at night. No ground-level photograph of this portal exists. It sits in a cut beside the Brookwood interchange, out of sight of public roads; the nearest Street View panorama shows only Bridge A's south abutment, a vertically ribbed cast wall that is consistent with the walls drawn here. The headwall form, its dimensions and every lamp are therefore illustrative. ## Island grates between Civic Center and North Avenue

North of Civic Center the line runs straight from tunnel into the enclosed station box, and on to North Avenue. OSM maps no portal or open cut there: every segment is `tunnel=yes`, and the station is an elevated hall over the Connector trench. Two ventilation shafts (OSM ways 1455666621 and 1455666622, about 5 × 4 m and 5 × 19 m) lie in the planted traffic island where West Peachtree Street, Ted Turner Drive and Pine Street meet, beside Maria Artemis's 1995 sculpture *Ex Static Test Program*. A 1990s Midtown Alliance photograph of the sculpture shows, at its edge, a dark grate nearly flush in a low concrete curb beside a planting bed. An April 2026 Street View panorama from Ted Turner Drive shows the island now shaded by trees. Both grates are drawn draped on the ground, 0.08 m proud, inside a 0.3 m curb standing 0.22 m high. The curb dimensions are estimates. No night photograph was found, so the grates are unlit.
