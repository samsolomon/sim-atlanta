# The two garages across Piedmont Avenue

On the approach to Georgia State, two pale rectangles behind the Grady wards used to read as office blocks. The **Grady Piedmont Deck** at 80 Piedmont Avenue SE is wrapped in brown brick, vertical concrete piers and metal screens. **Georgia State M Deck** at 33 Auditorium Place has long open slots, cream spandrels and a glazed stair tower at the corner. Correll’s own parking entrance is visible beside the Grady garage in the Piedmont Avenue photograph. Its building has a separate footprint. [Grady inventory, p. 15](https://www.gradyhealth.org/wp-content/uploads/HB-321-2D-GHS-REAL-ESTATE-INVENTORY-3.pdf#page=15) · [GSU parking directory](https://parking.gsu.edu/parking/parking-deck-addresses/) · [HOK’s Correll Pavilion](https://www.hok.com/projects/view/grady-health-system-correll-pavilion/)

## Footprints, levels and elevations

The two four- and five-corner footprints were sampled separately. The ground column is the range of the City bare-earth grid along the perimeter, sampled at 30 positions per edge. The roof column is the middle half of 2018 lidar elevations **inside that same polygon**, including split decks and vehicles. All elevations are metres NAVD88. The City model uses a level roof above its existing terrain foundation. Its parapet is a further 1.10 m. Grady’s inventory records ten floors and 1,430 staff spaces. The taller exterior divisions group roughly two floors at a time. On Armstrong, the screen extends down to a low brick plinth. On Piedmont, solid panels flank screened openings. The separate Correll bay sits beyond the deck’s end wall. The overhead shows several full rows and an enclosure at one roof corner. City places 26 illustrative parked sedans here. The Board of Regents approved M Deck’s expansion to seven parking levels. Its Auditorium corner has a glazed stair tower and the Gilmer side has long pale bands between wide piers. Six illustrative cars occupy the otherwise open, striped roof. Three low dividers follow the visible ramp edges. ## Nearby parcels

The campus directory lists S, K and N at 99 Gilmer. The facilities inventory separately identifies N at 110 Piedmont. Three roof outlines and their split parking surfaces are visible from above.
