[Open the silhouette sheet](silhouettes.svg). Both rows use one scale and the same blue-gray paint. The SVG is a design projection of the metre-scale meshes. City and street-eye screenshots are pending the separate capture window.

Dimensions are rounded study targets based on the [2018 Camry specifications](https://pressroom.toyota.com/2017-naias-detroit-toyota-camry/) and [2019 RAV4 specifications](https://pressroom.toyota.com/all-new-2019-toyota-rav4-serves-breakthrough-debut-nyias/). Roof taper, glass area, belt height, track, tyre radius, axle placement relative to the bumpers, lamp placement and overhangs are estimated from photographs. The crossover height is a roof estimate excluding rails.
