# Sedan and 589 finish prototypes

The comparison uses the same neutral blue-grey body paint on all four meshes. The original sedan is from the seven-body sheet and the original tractor is the lead option in the commercial sheet. The tractor is uncoupled so the rear frame, catwalk and fifth-wheel throat are visible.

## Sedan

The study uses a 2.825 m wheelbase, 0.975 m front overhang and 1.085 m rear overhang, giving a 4.885 m body. These dimensions come from the UK XV70 Camry specification. The Japanese-market daytime photos guide the body surfaces. The study combines those references across trims. The tyre radius, recess depth, mirror stalk and glazing angles are estimates. [Dimension table](https://media.toyota.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/pdf/1614162877210222MCamryFullRelease.pdf) · [front](https://global.toyota/pages/news/images/2017/07/10/1330/cmrh1707_04_s.jpg) · [side](https://global.toyota/pages/news/images/2017/07/10/1330/cmrh1707_17_s.jpg) · [rear](https://global.toyota/pages/news/images/2017/07/10/1330/cmrh1707_10_s.jpg)

The skirt rises over each tyre in the mesh. A dark return lines the arch; a dished wheel, five spokes and a separate hub sit inside it. The hood narrows into the lamp pockets and intake. Separate panes, door cuts, handles, stalk mirrors and the trunk recess provide street-eye detail.

## Conventional tractor

The Peterbilt 589-131 body-builder drawing sets the bumper-to-front-axle distance at 31 inches (0.787 m) and bumper-to-back-of-cab at 130.9 inches (3.325 m). Tractor wheelbase is measured to the tandem midpoint. The study uses the dealer-specified 280-inch (7.112 m) wheelbase and 72-inch (1.83 m) sleeper. Rear overhang is estimated at 1.51 m to the end of the frame from the tandem midpoint: in the 800 px side photograph the steer hub is near x=91, the tandem midpoint near x=649 and the frame end near x=768. The photographed truck is a separate configuration. Tank and coupling dimensions are also estimates. ## Distance treatment

Each mesh packs into one vertex-colour draw. The close sedan has 1,890 triangles and the close tractor has 4,150. The distant forms have 538 and 958 respectively, omitting fine fittings and simplifying wheel geometry. Native City comparisons use the distant forms; street-eye and Model Lab use the close forms. The study is isolated from the traffic renderer and has no fleet performance result.
