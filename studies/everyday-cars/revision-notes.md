# Revised sedan shape and tandem drive wheels

The sedan follows the Japanese-market July 2017 Toyota Camry G Leather Package (AXVH70). Toyota lists a 4.885 × 1.840 × 1.445 m envelope, 2.825 m wheelbase and 1.580/1.585 m front/rear tracks. The tyre radius of 0.335 m is rounded. The axle locations retain the earlier XV70 overhang split of 0.975 m front and 1.085 m rear. The Japanese catalogue independently confirms their combined length. [Toyota specification](https://toyota.jp/ucar/catalog/brand-TOYOTA/car-CAMRY/201707/10110733/)

The Toyota launch profile was scaled between the wheel centres: 340 pixels in a 620-pixel-wide copy represent 2.825 m. The rear glass meets a short raised deck; the nose drops below the belt line ahead of the front axle. The resulting mesh uses a crowned roof, tapered upper glass, distinct deck and hood sections, and lamps wrapping onto rounded bumper corners. The colour and trim of the source photographs vary. [Side silhouette](https://global.toyota/pages/news/images/2017/07/10/1330/cmrh1707_17_s.jpg) · [front corner](https://global.toyota/pages/news/images/2017/07/10/1330/cmrh1707_02_s.jpg) · [rear corner](https://global.toyota/pages/news/images/2017/07/10/1330/cmrh1707_10_s.jpg)

The previous tractor mesh had one tyre on each end of each axle. The revised tractor carries two single steers and eight drive tyres. At each end of the tandem axles, two 0.260 m tyre casings have centres 0.322 m apart, leaving 0.062 m of clear space. The outer tyres and hubs retain their positions. The drive tyre envelope spans 2.324 m on both revisions; mirrors and fenders extend farther. The geometry check covered both close and distant forms.
